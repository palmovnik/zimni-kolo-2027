# German-language cycling media: winter/spring road-cycling destinations

Research date: 2026-10-22. Method: WebSearch only. WebFetch failed with DNS errors (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND www.tour-magazin.de`), so every claim below comes from search-result snippets and summaries, not from reading the full articles. The shared search budget ran out before the Antalya, Morocco and Andalusia follow-up searches could run. German "quotes" are given as they appeared in the snippets. They may be slightly shortened, so check them against the source before publishing them.

Source-quality notes:
- **TOUR** (tour-magazin.de), **RennRad** (radsport-rennrad.de), **quäldich** (quaeldich.de) and **bike-x** are the strongest sources here.
- **biketrailspfannenstiel.ch** looks like machine-generated SEO content and is marked [low trust]. Use it only as a weak signal.
- Operator pages (ASI Reisen, Huerzeler) are commercial and marked [operator].

## Lanzarote
- Verdict: TOUR says that among the Canaries Lanzarote is the flatter island and Gran Canaria the mountainous, rugged one. Climbs are moderate and only rarely reach double-digit gradients. Wind can drain more energy than the climbs, so large sprockets are a must. — [TOUR: Saisonstart auf den Kanarischen Inseln Lanzarote und Gran Canaria](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-saisonstart-auf-den-kanarischen-inseln-lanzarote-und-gran-canaria/), Oct 2023
- Recommended bases: Puerto del Carmen, Las Caletas, Costa Teguise and Club La Santa. — same TOUR article, Oct 2023
- Bike rental: TOUR counts about a dozen rental stations on the Canaries, including free motion, Cyclo Canaria, Lanzarote Bike and Huerzeler. Six days cost about €110 (top models up to about €240). — same TOUR article, Oct 2023
- Huerzeler's station is in Costa Teguise, directly on the sea. Its sample route is a 100 km loop through the central national-park area to Playa Blanca and back. — [huerzeler.com: Lanzarote](https://huerzeler.com/en/inspiration/stationen-und-hotels/lanzarote) [operator], undated
- Terrain: the Famara massif in the north (highest point Peñas del Chache) and the Ajaches hills in the south. Climbs run up to about 10 km, from sea level to over 600 m. — [RennRad: Rennrad Trainingslager Lanzarote](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=157), date unknown (probably about 2017)
- Must-ride climbs:
  - Tabayesco road up to Peñas del Chache, known from Ironman Lanzarote; it is the highest paved road on the island. — [quäldich: Peñas del Chache](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/penas-del-chache/)
  - Mirador del Río from Haría, with the view over La Graciosa. — [quäldich: Femés / Lanzarote passes](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/femes/)
  - Femés from Playa Blanca (south-west side), the longest and hardest approach. — same quäldich page
- Classic loop: 113 km from Puerto Calero via Tabayesco and Haría to Mirador del Río. — [bike-x: Lanzarote – Auf das Dach der Insel](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/lanzarote-auf-das-dach-der-insel), 2016
- quäldich's "Große Lanzarote-Runde" is about 162 km with 2,481 m of climbing. It warns not to underestimate the mountains or the wind. — [quäldich: Die große Lanzarote-Runde](https://www.quaeldich.de/touren/die-grosse-lanzarote-runde/)
- Timanfaya: you cannot ride freely inside the national park itself. — [Bike-Magazin: Radfahren im Vulkanland](https://www.bike-magazin.de/touren/europa/radfahren-im-vulkanland/), date unknown
- Warning (English-language forum, for contrast): wind made rides around Playa Blanca hard and there is hardly any shelter; the poster preferred Gran Canaria or Tenerife. — [BikeRadar forum: Lanzarote](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12679558/lanzarote), date unknown
- Quote: „Lanzarote ist eine winterliche Zuflucht für ausdauersportliche Zugvögel“ (gist: a winter refuge for migrating endurance athletes, i.e. triathletes). — TOUR Kanaren article, Oct 2023

## Fuerteventura
- Verdict: TOUR calls it ideal for winter training when the constant wind takes a break. The mountains in the centre and south-west are the most interesting, and climbs are mostly moderate. — [TOUR: Vier Rennrad-Touren auf Fuerteventura](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/kanarische-inseln-vier-rennrad-touren-auf-fuerteventura/), Patrick Kunkel, 14 Jan 2022 (TOUR 2/2022)
- Roads are well built, but some sections have rough asphalt, so TOUR recommends 28 mm tyres. — same TOUR article, Jan 2022
- One week gives about 500 km and 7,000 m of climbing and covers almost the whole island. — [quäldich: Region Fuerteventura](https://www.quaeldich.de/regionen/fuerteventura/)
- Base and rental: Playitas Resort near Gran Tarajal rents its own yellow BMC road bikes. The author had only 2 windy days out of 6, with mostly cloudless sky. — [bike-components blog: Rennrad-Wintertraining auf Fuerteventura](https://www.bike-components.de/blog/fahren/rennrad_wintertraining_auf_fuerteventura/), date unknown
- Warning: on Fuerteventura you should measure performance "in headwind speed", not only in metres climbed. Some days are stormy. — summarised from search results citing the bike-components blog and [low trust] [biketrailspfannenstiel.ch](https://biketrailspfannenstiel.ch/auskunft/fuerteventura-radfahren-rennrad/)

## La Palma
- Verdict: TOUR describes it as a strong road-cycling island for riders in good form. Routes include a "Vulkan-Runde" of about 108 km with about 2,420 m of climbing. GPX files are free to download. — [TOUR: La Palma – Kanarische Inseln](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/spanien-la-palma-kanarische-inseln/), date unknown
- Must-ride: Roque de los Muchachos (about 2,400 m), with two approaches:
  - the long east approach from sea level at Santa Cruz de La Palma;
  - the north-west approach from Kiosko Brista at about 1,200 m.
  The road is in perfect condition, but near the summit there is always a risk of rockfall and larger stones on the road. — [quäldich: Roque de los Muchachos](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/roque-de-los-muchachos/)
- Bases and rental: bike rental and guided tours are available in Los Llanos, Puerto Naos and Tazacorte. — [Bike-Magazin: La Isla Bonita](https://www.bike-magazin.de/touren/europa/la-isla-bonita-die-gruenste-insel-der-kanaren/), date unknown
- A road bike can be rented "very cheaply" in Puerto Naos. — [gps-tour.info #34373](https://www.gps-tour.info/34373), old (about 2010)
- quäldich tour report: [Gastspiel am Vulkan](https://www.quaeldich.de/touren/gastspiel-am-vulkan/). The content was not captured.
- No German quote was captured verbatim.

## Costa Blanca (Calpe / Dénia)
- Verdict: according to RennRad, many pro teams and many amateurs now prefer the Costa Blanca to Mallorca for spring camps, because of more stable weather and a larger choice of routes. Daytime winter temperatures average 16–18 °C, with about 300 sunny days a year. — [RennRad: Costa Blanca mit dem Rennrad: Frühform](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/costa-blanca-informationen/), date unknown
- Roads are mostly in very good condition, with little motor traffic both on the coast and inland. Spring gives a very high chance of meeting pro teams. — [RennRad: Rennrad Trainingslager Costa Blanca](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=317), date unknown
- Hinterland climbs between Dénia and Calpe are mostly not very steep, though some are up to 15 km long. — same RennRad articles
- Routes recommended by hotel owner and guide Burkhard Jost (Dénia):
  - "Genuss-Runde" from Dénia via Vall de Gallinera to the Beniarrés reservoir: 115 km, 1,300 m, no steep sections, with a view of the Peñón de Ifach on the way back;
  - "Königsetappe": 140 km, 2,300 m, starting with Coll de Rates after a 20 km warm-up.
  The hotel name was not captured. — [RennRad: Costa Blanca, Action, Berge](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/touren-costa-blanca/), date unknown
- An editorial ride report from Dénia describes a guided hinterland ride to La Vall d'Ebo during almond blossom. — same RennRad Costa Blanca pages
- Coll de Rates is the region's classic and probably its most-ridden climb, sometimes used in the Vuelta. The north side from Parcent is about 6.6 km at about 5.2 %. A steep concrete track continues from the top towards Tossal dels Diners. — [quäldich: Coll de Rates](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/coll-de-rates/rennradklima/)
- Practical tips (generic, from TOUR):
  - reserve a rental bike early, because sizes run out;
  - rental insurance costs about €10–20 per week and does not cover theft, so never leave the bike unattended;
  - bring a saddle bag with tube and tools, because not every rental bike is fully equipped.
  — [TOUR: Mallorca & Co. – die besten Tipps fürs Trainingslager](https://www.tour-magazin.de/fitness/training/rennrad-trainingslager-mallorca-and-co-die-besten-tipps-fuers-trainingslager/), date unknown
- Warning (English source, for contrast): a Cambridge University club camp in Calpe in early 2026 had two days of rain on arrival, so winter weather is not guaranteed. — [CUCC training camp round-up](https://cucc.co.uk/2026/02/the-annual-cucc-training-camp-round-up/), Feb 2026
- Quote: „Auf 115 Kilometern und 1300 Höhenmetern sind keine steilen Hürden zu überwinden“ (gist: 115 km and 1,300 m with no steep obstacles, a relaxed "pleasure loop"). — RennRad Costa Blanca, date unknown

## Girona
- Verdict: a road-cycling hotspot and pro base. The key social ride is the open "Sunday Salida" run by the shop The Service Course (former pro Christian Meier and his wife Amber). The shop also rents high-end bikes, offers mechanics and runs guided rides. — [bike-x: Rennrad-Hotspot Girona](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/rennradtouren-rund-um-girona), 1 Jan 2020
- Café: La Fabrica in the old town is the riders' meeting point; people sit on the steps outside after rides. The same couple runs a roastery. — same bike-x article, 2020. The café's current status was not verified.
- Classic loops: the "Hincapie Sunset" loop with two climbs; "Els Àngels + Santa Pellaia" (53.7 km, moderate). — same bike-x article and [Komoot Girona guide](https://www.komoot.com/de-de/guide/26533/rennradtouren-in-girona)
- Must-ride: Rocacorba, south of Banyoles: 13.2 km, 792 m, 6.0 % average. It has little traffic, a hard upper part and great views, and is the region's favourite training climb. — [quäldich: Rocacorba](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/rocacorba/)
- Hotels:
  - Girona tourist office lists cycling-certified hotels: Hotel Carlemany, Hotel Ciutat de Girona, Hotel Gran Ultònia. — [Girona Turisme: DTE hotels](https://girona.cat/web/turisme/eng/dte_hotels.php) (non-German source)
  - Hotel Carlemany 4*, near the station, has a CCTV-secured bike room for up to 40 bikes. — [Costa Brava training camps: Hotel Carlemany](https://trainingcamps.costabrava.org/en/accomodation/1801/) (non-German source)
  - A Liv women's trip with a "relaxed rides, good food" theme is based at Hotel Can Bassa in Madremanya. — [Liv Cycling DE event](https://www.liv-cycling.com/de/events/female-cycling-vibes-relaxed-rides-good-food-in-girona/10151), Oct 2026
- Warning: no German source discussed winter weather. Girona is cooler than the southern destinations; season suitability for Jan–Feb was not covered in German sources (see Gaps).
- No German quote was captured verbatim.

## Andalusia (Málaga / Ronda / Marbella; Almería)
- Verdict: TOUR presents it as "the ideal Mallorca alternative". It offers:
  - coastal rides near Málaga with many sunshine hours and rolling terrain;
  - flat routes on the Costa de la Luz;
  - long climbs in the Sierra de Grazalema that demand good fitness;
  - little rain, except in the Sierra.
  — [TOUR: Andalusien als ideale Mallorca-Alternative](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-andalusien-als-ideale-mallorca-alternative/), 16 Oct 2023
- Bases named: Chiclana de la Frontera, Málaga, Torrox. Week packages with hotel and guides start at about €540 per person (double room, half board). — same TOUR article, Oct 2023
- Bike rental: fewer than a dozen stations, e.g. Huerzeler, Starbikes, ToroBike2.0, bike2malaga. Six days cost from about €120. Direct flights go to Málaga and Jerez (about 3 h from Germany). — same TOUR article, Oct 2023
- Best months: April to end of June, plus September–October. High summer is too hot. The Costa de la Luz allows training from February onwards. — [RennRad: Rennrad Trainingslager Andalusien](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/rennrad-trainingslager-andalusien/), date unknown
- Huerzeler has a station in Andalusia. — [RennRad: Huerzeler Radreisen](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/huerzeler-reisen-herbst-2024/), 2024 [operator promo]
- Ronda: Komoot lists a loop of about 48 km with 1,070 m between the Grazalema and Nieves sierras. No magazine article was found. — [Komoot: Rennradtouren rund um Ronda](https://www.komoot.com/de-de/guide/1623301/rennradtouren-rund-um-ronda)
- Almería: no German magazine coverage found.
  - Non-German sources: Alto de Velefique is about 13 km at about 8 % (a Vuelta 2021 "Especial" finish). — [Wilier Journal](https://journal.wilier.com/en/alto-de-velefique-the-first-finish-of-the-vuelta-2021)
  - Away from the coast you can ride 20–30 km without a shop or café; bring a jacket for the Calar Alto and Velefique descents. — [epicroadrides.com](https://epicroadrides.com/?p=14949)
- Quote: „Mallorca ist mit Abstand das beliebteste Revier fürs Rennradtraining, nicht nur unter Deutschen“, followed by the invitation to try something different (gist: Mallorca is by far the favourite, so why not try Andalusia?). — TOUR Andalusien, Oct 2023

## Cyprus
- Verdict: TOUR's spring season-opener. In March there are already more than 10 hours of sun a day and about 19 °C on the coast. Gradients are moderate: „Rampen jenseits der Zehn-Prozent-Marke stellen sich selten in den Weg“. — [TOUR: Saisonstart auf Zypern](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-saisonstart-auf-zypern/), 2023 ([English version](https://www.tour-magazin.de/en/europe/road-bike-training-camp-in-spring-start-of-the-season-in-cyprus/))
- Riding area: the south coast from Paphos via Pissouri and Limassol to west of Larnaca. — same TOUR article, 2023
- Packages and rental:
  - hotel plus guided-tour week packages start at €650;
  - Aspire Cycling (Paphos) charges €165 for 6 days on a carbon bike with Shimano 105.
  — same TOUR article, 2023
- Downsides named by TOUR: left-hand traffic, few rental providers, and flying is the only sensible way to get there. — same TOUR article, 2023
- Four TOUR routes, GPX included, all in the Republic of Cyprus:
  - "Auf den Olymp": 132 km, 3,000 m, max 12 %;
  - "Tour der Gegensätze": 89 km, 1,500 m;
  - "National Forest Park": 101 km, 2,300 m;
  - "Zum schwebenden Kreuz": 121 km, 1,600 m.
  — [TOUR: Frühjahrstouren mit dem Rennrad – Vier Rennrad-Touren auf Zypern](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/mittelmeerinsel-zypern-fruehjahrstouren-mit-dem-rennrad-vier-rennrad-touren-auf-zypern/), date unknown
- Must-ride: Olympos (Troodos). The summit itself is closed (a British radar station since 1974), but a very good asphalt road goes up to about 1,938 m. The most popular approach is from Trimiklini via Troodos Square: about 21 km and 1,400 m. — [quäldich: Olympos](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/olympos/)
- Warning: there can still be snow on top in spring, and it is cool above about 2,000 m. Always carry warm clothes and rain gear into the Troodos. — quäldich Olympos / [Rennradklima](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/olympos/rennradklima/)
- Hotels:
  - Columbia Beach Hotel, Pissouri, was the start point of a trade-press ride report. Barely any car overtook the riders on the small inland roads. — [touristik-aktuell: Konkurrenz für Mallorca](https://www.touristik-aktuell.de/reportagen/europa/konkurrenz-fuer-mallorca/), date unknown
  - Aldiana club near Alaminos was used as a base via the operator Zypernbike. — [bike-x: Zypern – Rennrad- und MTB-Insel der Extra-Klasse](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/zypern-rennrad-und-mtb-insel-der-extra-klasse), 27 Jan 2011 (old)
- Season: RennRad cites 320 sunny days a year and says the south/south-east is the best camp base; it gives sample routes of 84 and 120 km around Limassol and Larnaca. — [RennRad: Rennrad Trainingslager Zypern](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/rennrad-trainingslager-zypern/), date unknown
- Season, Swiss operator bikeCyprus: spring window from mid-February to mid-June for 2026/27. — [bikecyprus.ch](https://bikecyprus.ch/) [operator]
- Forum: a Rennrad-News thread asking about Cyprus camps got only a one-line answer ("was there in October"). — [Rennrad-News forum post](https://www.rennrad-news.de/forum/goto/post?id=6325201)
- Quote: „Rampen jenseits der Zehn-Prozent-Marke stellen sich selten in den Weg“ (gist: ramps over 10 % rarely get in your way). — TOUR Zypern, 2023

## Madeira
- Verdict: only for climbers. TOUR: whoever wants to win races there must be able to climb; climbs are numerous and very steep. A rental shop warns customers not to come unless they are very fit. — [TOUR: Madeira mit dem Rennrad](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/madeira-portugal-urlaub-mit-dem-rennrad-touren-auf-der-insel/), date unknown
- Old TOUR report: the rider called the climbs "brutal". The Porto da Cruz to Portela ramp is 6 km at 10 %, and he ended up pushing. — [TOUR: Rennrad – Portugal: Madeira](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/portugal-rennrad-portugal-madeira/), 2003 (old)
- Climbs:
  - Cabo Girão: three hard kilometres well into double digits;
  - Achada do Teixeira: one of the hardest on the island.
  — [quäldich: Cabo Girão](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/cabo-girao/); [quäldich: Achada do Teixeira](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/achada-do-teixeira/rennradklima/)
- Route idea from a user comment: take the cable car to Monte, ride up to Pico do Arieiro, return via Poiso and down to Funchal. — [quäldich: Pico do Arieiro comments](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/pico-do-arieiro/kommentare/)
- Rental: Madeira Bergziegen (German owner) rents good MTBs and road bikes. — same quäldich comments, date unknown
- Quote: „Wer auf der portugiesischen Insel im Atlantik Rennen gewinnen will, muss klettern können“ (gist: on Madeira you have to be able to climb). — TOUR Madeira, date unknown

## Algarve
- Verdict: RennRad: „Hügel, Strände, Ruhe, leere Straßen: Die Algarve bietet extrem viel – und ist im Radsport fast noch ein Geheimtipp.“ — [RennRad: Meer Sonne (Algarve)](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=21180), date unknown (2020s)
- Conditions: a mid-February trip reported 15–25 °C, and most of the narrow, often empty roads were freshly paved. Local guides from Bikesul led rides. Bikesul, based in Albufeira, is one of the largest cycling providers on the Algarve and runs a "Volta ao Algarve Experience". — same RennRad article, and [RennRad: Algarve Granfondo preview](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=19305)
- Event: the Algarve Granfondo, from Lagoa in February, runs alongside the Volta ao Algarve, with 50, 86 or 132 km distances. The Fóia summit stage is about 16 km with about 780 m. — RennRad Granfondo preview, date unknown
- Must-ride: Fóia (902–907 m depending on source), the highest point of the Algarve, via Monchique. Sample loop: Portimão–Lagoa–Monchique–Fóia, 92 km and 1,143 m. Take climbing gears. — [RennRad: Reiseziel Trainingslager](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/reise/reiseziel-trainingslager/), date unknown; [quäldich: Fóia](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/foia/)
- Fóia is the more touristy of the two Monchique peaks (Fóia, Picota). Winter and spring bring calm, deserted roads. — RennRad [?p=10649](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=10649), date unknown
- Packages: ASI Reisen sells an 8-day road tour from €1,040 per person (double room). — [ASI Reisen: Bike Algarve Winter](https://www.asi-reisen.de/tdi/bike-algarve-winter/2) [operator]
- Quote: see the verdict above ("fast noch ein Geheimtipp" = still almost an insider tip).

## Sicily (Etna)
- Verdict: TOUR published 4 routes with GPX, including:
  - a Madonie loop: about 120 km, 2,100 m;
  - an Etna loop: 122 km, 2,500 m, max 10 %.
  — [TOUR: Italien – Sizilien (mit GPS-Daten)](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/italien/italien-sizilien-mit-gps-daten/), date unknown (older)
- Etna (quäldich): paved roads climb from several sides, the highest reaching about 2,000 m at Rifugio Sapienza. Every road is subject to change from lava flows. The Zafferana side climbs fairly evenly at 6–8 %. — [quäldich: Ätna](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/aetna/)
- Rider comment (Oct 2023), on a loop Nicolosi–Zafferana–Rifugio Sapienza–Nicolosi: many long steep sections on sometimes rough surfaces. He wished for 34/32 gearing and recommends wide tyres for the descent. — [quäldich: Ätna comments](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/aetna/kommentare/), Oct 2023
- Conflict: a [low trust] page calls the Zafferana climb about 19 km at "mostly double digits", which contradicts quäldich's 6–8 %. — [biketrailspfannenstiel.ch](https://biketrailspfannenstiel.ch/auskunft/radfahren-sizilien-rennrad/)
- Madonie: Piano Battaglia, starting from Cefalù, has a relative height comparable to a higher Alpine pass. The author met very little traffic, with barely a handful of cyclists in 3 days. — [quäldich: Piano Battaglia](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/piano-battaglia/)
- No hotels, restaurants or rental shops were named in the German sources found.
- No German quote was captured verbatim.

## Crete
- Verdict: quäldich: Crete has mountains "galore" up to about 2,500 m, the east is recommended for hobby riders, roads have improved considerably, and the region page lists 35 passes. — [quäldich: Region Kreta](https://www.quaeldich.de/regionen/kreta/)
- Passes:
  - Imbros (821 m): road very well built for Cretan standards; combining it with the Asfendos branch gives about 1,100 m in one go from the south coast. — [quäldich: Imbros-Pass](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/imbros-pass/rennradklima/)
  - Theriso (715 m) from Chania: a shady gorge; loose stones after heavy rain. — [quäldich: Theriso](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/theriso-meskla/)
  - Drakona (705 m): "a very good taverna" in the village (name not captured). — [quäldich: Drakona-Pass](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/drakona-pass/)
  - Malaxa (450 m): a low-traffic alternative to the EO90 coastal road. — [quäldich: Malaxa](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/malaxa/)
  - Meterizi (790 m), Kissamos–Paleochora via the Topolia gorge. — [quäldich: Meterizi](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/meterizi-pass/)
- Psiloritis massif: Skinakas (1,737 m) and other passes. — [quäldich: Psiloritis-Massiv](https://www.quaeldich.de/regionen/psiloritis-massiv/)
- Rental (German-language Tripadvisor reviews): Kreta Bike was praised for professional mechanics; a rented Scott bike was well maintained; one reviewer has done a third camp on Crete and finds it "more original and hospitable than Mallorca". — [Tripadvisor DE: Rethymnon equipment rentals](https://www.tripadvisor.de/Attractions-g1852993-Activities-c61-t186-Rethymnon_Prefecture_Crete.html), date unknown
- Season: many Cretan passes have too few logged rides on quäldich to determine a main season. Best months were stated only by a [low trust] source (April–May, Sep–Oct).
- No German quote was captured verbatim.

## Marrakech / High Atlas
- Only operator material was found. ASI Reisen's "Marokko – Mit dem Rennrad vom Atlas in die Wüste":
  - 9 days, from €1,945, groups of 1–12;
  - 7 riding stages, 4–6 h a day, "regular ups and downs";
  - crosses Tizi n'Tichka (2,260 m) with a long descent into the rain shadow;
  - continues via the Dadès gorge, Aït Benhaddou and Zagora.
  Some page versions name Tizi n'Test or Tizi n'Tazazert instead. — [ASI Reisen EPHA30AQ0000](https://www.asi-reisen.de/r/EPHA30AQ0000) (2027 version: [link](https://www.asi-reisen.de/r/EPHA30AQ0000/v/2027)) [operator]
- quäldich has an Atlas region page listing Tizi n'Tichka (2,260 m) and Tizi n'Test (2,093 m). — [quäldich: Atlas](https://www.quaeldich.de/regionen/atlas/)
- This is a point-to-point tour, not a hub stay. No independent experiences were found on traffic, dogs or road quality.

## Tuscany
- Verdict: spring and autumn are best; summer is often too hot. There are no high mountains, but the constant up and down piles up climbing. — [bike-x: Alle Infos zum Rennrad-Paradies Toskana](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/kurz-und-kompakt-alle-infos-zum-rennrad-paradies-toskana), 2009 (old)
- Strade Bianche gravel needs a different technique: balance with the upper body and keep tension when climbing so the rear wheel keeps traction. — [RennRad: Staub & Steine (Strade Bianche Granfondo)](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/race/strade-bianche-granfondo-toskana/), date unknown
- Komoot Strade Bianche loops run about 144 km with about 2,200 m. — [Komoot: Rennradtouren in Siena](https://www.komoot.com/de-de/guide/59697/rennradtouren-in-siena)
- No German source covered winter (Jan–Mar) riding conditions or named hotels or restaurants.

## Georgia (Kakheti)
- Gombori Pass (1,620 m) connects Tbilisi with the Kakheti wine region. The climb starts at the roundabout by the Gombori patrol police station, about 42 km to the top. quäldich has too few logged rides to give a season. — [quäldich: Gombori-Pass](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/gombori-pass/)
- For climbers: Abano / Torgva pass (2,862 m), from Pshaveli about 38 km and 2,400 m. Note on the border situation as of 2020. Not a winter option. — [quäldich: Abano-Pass](https://www.quaeldich.de/paesse/abano-pass/)
- The Georgian tourism site warns of fog or snow on Gombori in autumn. — [georgia.travel](https://georgia.travel/en_US/article/gombori-pass-picturesque-road-to-kakheti) (non-German source)
- No German source addressed Jan–Apr suitability. A high pass in winter is likely problematic, but that is not stated by any source.

## Jordan
- Only operator and platform material was found. ASI Reisen's Dead Sea to Red Sea trip is 9 days from €2,595, 6–14 people, and about 30 % off-road on mountain bikes. It is not suitable for road bikes. — [ASI Reisen EPHA38AD0000](https://www.asi-reisen.de/r/EPHA38AD0000) [operator]
- Bikemap (German site) calls Jordan one of the safest countries in the Middle East, with quiet roads and dry weather. It warns of steep roads with tight bends and blind spots that need constant braking. — [Bikemap: Jordanien](https://www.bikemap.net/de/l/248816), date unknown

## UAE (Ras Al Khaimah / Jebel Jais)
- No German-language source was found.
- Non-German sources:
  - Jebel Jais: 35 km at an even 4.1 %, little traffic, ridden in March from RAK early in the morning; mountain goats can cross on the descent. — [Café du Cycliste: Jebel Jais](https://cafeducycliste.com/en/la-gazette/jebel-jais-a-mountain-in-the-desert), date unknown
  - An 80 km "Grand Loop" is under development. — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/uaes-highest-peak-jebel-jais-gets-80km-grand-loop-and-5-day-climbing-trail)

## Oman
- Best time is October–March, with winter days around 25 °C. The road network is very good for desert riding. — [bike-x: Winter in der Wüste](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/winter-in-der-wueste), about 2010 (old)
- TOUR ran a bikepacking feature (two women riding through Oman). Details were not captured. — [TOUR: Reise Oman – Bikepacking](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/fernziele/reise-oman-bikepacking-im-osten-der-arabischen-halbinsel-zwei-frauen-radeln-durch-den-oman/), date unknown
- Jebel Akhdar:
  - the first 14 km averaged 10.3 % at the Haute Route Oman 2020;
  - reports of a police checkpoint allowing only 4x4 vehicles up, so access for bikes is unclear;
  - the descent is hard on brakes.
  — [Haute Route Oman report](https://www.hauteroute.org/news/view/an-epic-first-day-at-the-second-edition-of-haute-route-oman) (non-German)

## Mallorca (reference)
- Mallorca is the benchmark: most cyclists train there from February to May. — [TOUR: Sechs europäische Alternativen zu Mallorca](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-sechs-europaeische-alternativen-zu-mallorca/), Oct 2023
- Crowds: on fine days „sitzen hier mittags mehrere Hundert Radsportler bei Kaffee und Mandelkuchen“ (Petra square; gist: hundreds of cyclists at lunchtime over coffee and almond cake). For quiet riding go to the Serres de Llevant in the north-east, though Artà's square is busy. — [TOUR: Schöne Rennradtouren auf Mallorca](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/mallorca-rennrad-urlaub-schone-rennradtouren-auf-mallorca/), date unknown
- Rules: keep right and use the paved shoulder, which may have embedded reflectors. The rule is lifted on descents. Groups may ride two abreast only on wide roads, and a group counts as one vehicle at junctions. — [TOUR: Rennrad-Touren Mallorca – Tipps](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/rennrad-touren-mallorca-tipps-strecken-and-die-wichtigsten-infos/), date unknown
- TOUR's 2008 test of operator camps had winners Max Hürzeler and Luxcom. Hotels, food, workshops and rental bikes drew little criticism. — [TOUR: Trainingslager-Test Mallorca](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/trainingslager-test-mallorca/), 2008 (old)

## Tenerife (reference)
- TOUR: from sea level up to the Las Cañadas caldera (over 2,000 m); about 2,000 m of climbing in one go is tough. Bora-hansgrohe trains there in winter. A gentler starter loop runs from Los Cristianos. — [TOUR: Im Höhenrausch am Pico del Teide](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/teneriffa-6-traum-touren-im-schatten-des-teide-mit-dem-rennrad/), date unknown

## Gran Canaria (reference)
- TOUR: "mountainous and wild", about 20 °C average already in January. 100 km loops without lots of climbing are hardly possible. — [TOUR Kanaren](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/spanien/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-saisonstart-auf-den-kanarischen-inseln-lanzarote-und-gran-canaria/), 13 Oct 2023
- RennRad: the Mogán valley climb is "certainly one of the most beautiful" on the island; the south is usually less windy than the north. — [RennRad: Rennrad Trainingslager Gran Canaria](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=315), date unknown

## General tips (all destinations)
- Arrive with about 1,000 km in your legs, and don't overdo it in the first days. Guided groups mean more drafting and quiet roads. — [RennRad: Saisonvorbereitung / Trainingslager](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=6488), date unknown
