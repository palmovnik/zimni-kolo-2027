# Rider experiences: winter/spring road-cycling destinations

Compiled 2026-10-22 for a 4–5 night trip (Jan–Apr 2027) by 5–6 Czech road cyclists using rental bikes.

**How this was gathered, and its limits**
- Every source was reached through WebSearch result snippets only. WebFetch failed with DNS errors (road.cc, cyclechat.net), so no thread was read in full.
- Reddit is blocked for the search tool (HTTP 400), so there is **no Reddit material** in these notes.
- Quotes are the wording shown in the search snippets and were not checked against the full page.
- Many forum posts are old, from 2008 to 2019. Where the snippet showed a date it is given; otherwise the bullet says "date unknown".
- "(single anecdote)" means one person's experience.
- The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Some locations are therefore thin; see Gaps.

## Lanzarote
- **Wind is the main complaint.** One rider had a steady northerly of about 20 mph (32 km/h). Because "the only way out of PdC [Puerto del Carmen] is by climbing in a northerly direction", this limited the rides they could do. Another rider lost two days to severe wind in October and found crosswinds gusting to 40 mph "not fun". — [Singletrack "Bloody windy on Lanzarote"](https://singletrackworld.com/forum/bike-forum/bloody-windy-on-lanzarote/), [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12553441/lanzarote), old threads, date unknown
- **Best months (forum opinion):** "Lanzarote is great for cycling in November/December as it isn't generally so windy then". Early spring is also suggested. — [BikeRadar Lanzarote thread](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12951606/lanzarote), date unknown
- **Roads:** riders describe smooth roads with "pretty quiet roads but always a wind to contend with". — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12794780/lanzarote), date unknown
- **Difficulty:** CycleChat riders call it "really challenging", mostly because of the wind and the size of some hills. — [CycleChat "Winter Cycling Destination?"](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/winter-cycling-destination.310901/latest), date unknown
- **Club La Santa:** praised as "probably the best facility of it's kind in Europe, from a triathlete's point of view", with on-site bike hire, a mechanic and free guided rides. Caveat from another forum: "unless you are staying there you will need a rental car to get to that side of the Island (west coast)". On the quality of the hire bikes, one reply pointed to a local shop instead, Pro Bike, "just down the road from CLS". — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/14674246/), [Slowtwitch](https://forum.slowtwitch.com/t/lanzarote-recommendations/785110), dates unknown
- **Named rentals (good experiences):**
  - A Trek Madone 3.1 from Lanzarote Cycling (lanzarote-cycling.com) was dropped off at and collected from the hotel.
  - A Merida Ride 94 from Cool Bikes, Playa Blanca, cost €20/day and was "a nice bike and presented in good condition" (single anecdote).
  - Renner Bikes in Puerto del Carmen is mentioned in several threads.
  - All of these posts are old, so check that the shops still exist. — [BikeRadar threads](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/13042797/lanzarote), date unknown
- **Vulcan Bike:** no independent reviews were found. One German affiliate-style guide only lists "Vulcan Bike Rental" as reliable. — [biketrailspfannenstiel.ch](https://biketrailspfannenstiel.ch/auskunft/lanzarote-rennrad-leihen/), date unknown
- **Tips:**
  - Ask for low gears. One rider found a TT bike a hassle on the climbs.
  - Bring arm warmers and a gilet or windshell even in spring.
  - Popular rides are Playa Blanca–Femés and Mirador del Río. Mirador del Río is "mostly windy".
  - German source: "große Ritzel Pflicht" (big sprockets are a must), and there is almost no shade.
  - — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12553441/lanzarote), [radsport-rennrad.de](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=157), dates unknown
- **Quote:** "Last time we were there we had a steady northerly of around 20mph on average." — [Singletrack](https://singletrackworld.com/forum/bike-forum/bloody-windy-on-lanzarote/)

## Fuerteventura
- **What it trades:** the island "swaps the climbing of Gran Canaria for wind".
  - Base yourself in **Corralejo** (north).
  - Do not ride south with the prevailing wind and then grind back into it. Plan loops so the windy leg comes first.
  - — [CycleChat "Winter Cycling Destination?"](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/winter-cycling-destination.310901/post-7626847), date unknown
- **Wind strength:** one rider on a winter trip reported near-constant wind of about 30 mph with gusts to 45. A 150 km ride "felt like a slog" (single anecdote). — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19864952/), date unknown
- **Climbs and surfaces:** the interior climbs are moderate. Fit **28 mm tyres** because some tarmac is rough ("rollen gut und schonen Nerven und Handgelenke", they roll well and spare your nerves and wrists). — [TOUR magazin](https://www.tour-magazin.de/en/tours/spain/canary-islands-four-road-bike-tours-on-fuerteventura/), editorial, date unknown
- **Rentals:** no first-hand reviews of road-bike hire were found.
  - Easy Riders (Corralejo) is praised for knowledgeable, low-pressure staff, but the one forum report (2019) was about MTB hire.
  - — [Singletrack "MTB in Fuerteventura"](https://singletrackworld.com/forum/bike-forum/mtb-in-fuertaventura), 2019; [travel-buddies.com](https://travel-buddies.com/e-bike-rentals-in-corralejo-fuerteventura/), date unknown
- **Quote:** "swaps the climbing of Gran Canaria for wind" — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/winter-cycling-destination.310901/post-7626847)

## La Palma
- **First-hand reports:** none found. One TrainerRoad forum post calls it "the steepest island in the world" and says Roque de los Muchachos climbs at about 7.5% from sea level to about 2,400 m. — [TrainerRoad forum](https://www.trainerroad.com/forum/t/cycling-getaway-for-winters-where-are-you-going/78192?page=3), date unknown
- **Winter conditions:** a Komoot tip warns that snow at the summit is not uncommon in winter. Check weather and road status before riding. — [Komoot highlight 380643](https://blog.komoot.de/highlight/380643), date unknown
- **Red flag, reported by PJAMM and not verified:** upper LP-4 resurfacing, with weekday lane closures since late July 2026. Check before riding on a weekday. — [PJAMM Roque de los Muchachos East](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/428.Roque%2520de%2520Los%2520Muchacho), 2026
- **Weather on the east side:** CyclingSpain (an operator) says Dec–Feb has 1–2 rainy days on the east side and about 18 °C at sea level. — [cyclingspain.com](https://www.cyclingspain.com/inspiration/cycling-la-palma/)
- **Quote:** "the steepest island in the world" — [TrainerRoad forum](https://www.trainerroad.com/forum/t/cycling-getaway-for-winters-where-are-you-going/78192?page=3)

## Costa Blanca (Calpe / Dénia)
- **Weather can disappoint.**
  - Pro-team camp report: a morning went from sun to "heavy rain, three degrees (yes, three), and gusty winds", and rides had to be postponed. — [Road Cycling UK](https://roadcyclinguk.com/sportive/training-camp-vitus-vitesse-evo-vri-first-ride-review.html), date unknown
  - A personal blog about a spring week had rain forecast on 5 of 7 days: "every other ride ended with … riding the last 5–10 miles back in pouring, driving rain" (single anecdote). — [To Be Determined, "The ill-fated, yet still fun, bike vacation to Calpe"](https://www.tobedetermined.cc/journal/riding-calpe-spain-training), around April, year unknown
- **Training versus holiday:** on BikeRadar, one member said Calpe/Dénia are good for serious training, while Mallorca suits a cycling *holiday* better. Another reply called Costa Blanca "the most popular cycling destination in mainland Spain" in that season. — [BikeRadar "End of January – Calpe, Girona or Mallorca…?"](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/20041555/), date unknown
- **Cafés (Komoot user tips):**
  - **Velosol Cycling Café, Xaló** is "a must for cyclists". It has good coffee and a repair workshop, and 105 of 115 Komoot users recommend it.
  - **Coll de Rates restaurant** has a "killer view from the patio" and "a fantastic strudel".
  - **Café de Rutes** on Coll de Rates is Austrian-run. It may be the same business as the restaurant; check.
  - The area had a forest fire; the restaurant survived.
  - — [Komoot 174183](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/highlight/174183), [Komoot 164500](https://blog.komoot.de/highlight/164500), [CycleBlaze "Calpe – Take Two"](https://www.cycleblaze.com/journals/Mockup/calpe-take-two/), 2016–2023
- **Rentals (named):**
  - Blancabikes has branches in Calpe N/S, Parcent, Jávea and Dénia. It asks for photo ID and possibly an accommodation receipt or the card used for payment.
  - Vuelta Turística is a Belgian-run hotel-based camp renting Specialized Tarmac/Roubaix, which can also be hired separately.
  - SolyBike is said to have a good reputation.
  - Biking Luca gets praise in reviews for repairs and fair prices.
  - No independent customer complaints were found.
  - — [advcollective listing](https://app.advcollective.com/local-experts/race-ready-cervelo-soloist-rental-in-calpe-south), [cyclingspain.com rentals](https://www.cyclingspain.com/do-it-yourself/bike-rentals-calpe-costa-blanca), [Wanderlog Biking Luca](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/10055991/biking-luca), [TUI.nl / Vuelta Turistica](https://www.tui.nl/sports/wielrennen/tussen-de-profs-en-cross-benidorm/)
- **Mixed traveller opinions:** on a Dutch review site, one reviewer rode daily and loved it, while another found Calpe "absoluut geen aanrader" (absolutely not recommended). The second review was not cycling-specific. — [27vakantiedagen.nl](https://www.27vakantiedagen.nl/?p=1029573)
- **German tips:**
  - Hinterland climbs between Dénia and Calpe are long but rarely steep. — [radsport-rennrad.de](https://www.radsport-rennrad.de/?p=317)
  - Reserve a rental early to get the right size. Consider rental insurance (about €10–20/week), which usually excludes theft. Bring your own saddle bag with tube and tools. — [TOUR magazin](https://www.tour-magazin.de/fitness/training/rennrad-trainingslager-mallorca-and-co-die-besten-tipps-fuers-trainingslager/)
- **Quote:** "heavy rain, three degrees (yes, three), and gusty winds" — [RCUK](https://roadcyclinguk.com/sportive/training-camp-vitus-vitesse-evo-vri-first-ride-review.html)

## Girona
- **Weather:**
  - Feb is "mostly mild… around the 60s F" (about 15–20 °C), but one February trip swung from 35 to 85 °F (about 2 to 29 °C). — [GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/biking/girona-spain-cyclist-bike-paradise), date unknown
  - January is the coldest month and chilly early, but it reaches double figures by about 11:00. The same operator blog gives night minimums of 2–6 °C in February. — [Eat Sleep Cycle blog](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/girona-winter-season-base-miles/), operator
- **Crowds and prices:** the off-season is about Oct to mid-March. "By March, tourists begin to infiltrate the city, and roads become more crowded as prices skyrocket." — [GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/biking/girona-spain-cyclist-bike-paradise)
- **Rental shops:**
  - **Bike Breaks closed in July 2021**, so ignore old recommendations for it. — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/girona-bike-tours/)
  - **Gironaroads:** no reviews found.
  - Aliveness Cycling (via Magpie) charges small extra deposits: €10 for the tube pouch and €15 for a pump or CO2. — [Magpie](https://magpie.travel/things-to-do/bike-rental-in-girona/455d18ff-37d5-42bf-af6a-c1069f7a639e)
- **Where to stay:**
  - Komoot reviews praise Rocacorba Cycling House: "fantastic breakfast, and optional dinners by Chef Ryan", with rental, workshop and guided rides. — [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/user/rocacorba)
  - CycleChat members suggest a second base near Berga for bigger passes. — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/fixed-base-cycling-in-girona-area.45921/latest), date unknown
- **Els Àngels (Komoot tips):**
  - The climb from Girona is longer but easier; from Madremanya it is shorter but harder.
  - It "can get busy with traffic, best first thing in the morning".
  - Descending to Madremanya, the road is "super bumpy".
  - — [Komoot highlights](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/7197204)
- **Flights:** Barcelona is the usual gateway; direct winter flights to Girona are limited (UK perspective). — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/20041555/)
- **Quote:** "By March, tourists begin to infiltrate the city, and roads become more crowded as prices skyrocket." — [GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/biking/girona-spain-cyclist-bike-paradise)

## Andalusia
- **Ronda as a base:** a road.cc forum poster calls Ronda a good base about 45 min from Málaga. Both Ronda and Granada "can get chilly in March or April". — [road.cc forum "An alternative to Mallorca – Granada Andalusia?"](https://road.cc/forums/topic/267384-alternative-mallorca-granada-andalusia), date unknown
- **Coast versus interior:** a touring cyclist in November 2019 left the cold, wet interior for the coast, which was about 15 degrees warmer. Near Málaga the land is "flatter, agricultural, and more congested"; El Chorro and the Ronda mountains are about 30 miles away. — [CycleBlaze "To Málaga"](https://www.cycleblaze.com/journals/Iberia2019/to-malaga/), Nov 2019
- **Drivers:** a winter tour found drivers patient, and the riders were among few tourists in small towns. — [teamkaker.com](https://teamkaker.com/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/2204), date unknown
- **Weather risk:** a Christmas tour (Algeciras–Ronda–Antequera) was stuck for two nights in Gaucín by bad weather (single anecdote, old). — [uv.es](https://www.uv.es/pitarque/mtb/and'.html)
- **Rentals:** no shop reviews were found.
- **Quote:** "can get chilly in March or April" — [road.cc](https://road.cc/forums/topic/267384-alternative-mallorca-granada-andalusia)

## Cyprus
- **Drivers (forum views differ):**
  - An ex-resident never had problems around Limassol, but warns that drivers run lights when roads are empty early and late.
  - Others call Paphos and Nicosia drivers "a bit poor… eager to get to the next bumper or junction".
  - — [BikeRadar "Cycling in Cyprus"](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12582441/cycling-in-cyprus), [CycleChat "Cyprus cycling"](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cyprus-cycling.234649/), dates unknown
- **Quiet roads:** one rider saw only about 10 cars in a 90-minute climb from Paphos toward Troodos. The coastal road Paphos–Limassol is quieter now the motorway is finished. — same threads
- **Winter and spring conditions:**
  - A touring cyclist in the cold months had "chill winds, gale force at times" and thick snow on the highest Troodos road.
  - Around Easter there can still be snow at the top of Troodos while the coast is 15–20 °C.
  - — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12582441/cycling-in-cyprus), [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cyprus-cycling.234649/)
- **Rentals:**
  - Shops lean to MTB and hybrids. **Ride Easy Bike Shop, Paphos** is named for good road rentals. — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-cyprus/), guide
  - A German forum user asked about a Feb/March camp using rental bikes; the only visible reply was from someone who went in October. — [rennrad-news.de forum](https://www.rennrad-news.de/forum/goto/post?id=6325201)
- **Red flags (German magazines):**
  - Left-hand traffic.
  - Stones on the road on descents ("nicht selten liegen Steine auf der Straße", stones on the road are not unusual).
  - — [TOUR magazin](https://www.tour-magazin.de/touren/europa/rennrad-trainingslager-im-fruehjahr-saisonstart-auf-zypern/), [bike-x.de](https://www.bike-x.de/rennrad/touren/zypern-rennrad-und-mtb-insel-der-extra-klasse)
- **Dogs:** no reports found either way.
- **Quote:** "all a bit eager to get to the next bumper or junction" — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cyprus-cycling.234649/)

## Madeira
- **Steepness:** a blogger had to walk parts of the São Vicente–Santana climb (single anecdote). CycleChat says there are only a couple of level stretches on the whole island. — [travellingclaus.com](https://travellingclaus.com/cycling-around-madeira/), [CycleChat "info on madeira"](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/info-on-madeira.10353/latest), old
- **Red flag: tunnels and drivers.**
  - The expressways have "narrow, dark tunnels where drivers will not be expecting to come across cyclists".
  - Drivers are "far more reckless since their newer, faster roads have opened".
  - — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/info-on-madeira.10353), old thread
  - Mountain roads are narrow; there is little space if you meet a car on a descent. — [road.cc](https://road.cc/?p=28147)
- **Red flag: rental gearing.** A Climbfinder commenter checked **three Funchal rental shops and none had low enough gearing** for Pico do Arieiro. The rental staff advised against the direct route. PJAMM recommends 34×30 or easier. — [Climbfinder Pico do Arieiro](https://climbfinder.com/us/climbs/pico-do-arieiro-casas), [PJAMM](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/424.Pico%20Arieiro), date unknown
- **Climb:** Pico do Arieiro from Funchal is about 18 km at 9.8% average, with an opening of about 20%. The route via Monte and Poiso averages about 8.3%. — [PJAMM](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/424.Pico%20Arieiro)
- **Quote:** "narrow, dark tunnels where drivers will not be expecting to come across cyclists" — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/info-on-madeira.10353)

## Algarve
- **Where to ride:**
  - The best riding is inland, north of the N125 (and the A22): "Many of these roads are very quiet and really well surfaced".
  - Monchique (Fóia) is worth doing because you start near sea level.
  - The N125 has a bad road-safety reputation, although this poster had no incidents.
  - — [BikeRadar "Lagos, Algarve in Feb 2017"](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19997973/), Feb 2017
- **Guided rides:** one rider enjoyed a guided Monchique ride and praised the guide; the operator also arranges hire with pickup. — [BikeRadar "Albufeira rentals"](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/13061536/albufeira-rentals), date unknown
- **Rentals (named, review aggregators):**
  - **Abílio Bikes (Tavira)** has "glowing reviews", bikes fitted to height with patch kit and pump, and a group of 25 found everything ready. The excerpts are mostly about non-road bikes.
  - **Algarve Cycling (Faro area):** a carbon road bike was "a great fit" with no pushy upselling. One complaint was loose gears, with the chain rattling on the derailleur.
  - — [Wanderlog Abílio](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/7828948/abílio-bikes-shop--rentals), [Wanderlog Algarve Cycling](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/681307/algarve-cycling)
- **Quote:** "The best riding is inland North of the N125… Many of these roads are very quiet and really well surfaced." — [BikeRadar](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19997973/), 2017

## Sicily (Etna)
- **North side:** a reader of Epic Road Rides rode it before the 2020 Giro and found the tarmac "perfect". It gets harder past Rifugio Ragabo, with about 400 m of climbing in 3–4 km (single anecdote, one day of riding). — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-mount-etna-sicily/), around 2020
- **South side:** a Komoot tip says the surface is "sometimes very bumpy" and smoother toward Nicolosi, so the tipster suggests riding it in that direction. Another tip mentions rough black lava-stone paving in town streets. — [Komoot smarttour](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/43066911), Sept 2017
- **Above Rifugio Sapienza:** volcanic gravel and cinder, not rideable on a road bike. PJAMM also reports an ongoing eruptive phase in 2026, so roads can close at short notice. — [PJAMM Mount Etna](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/140.Biking-Mount-Etna)
- **Coastal road near Augusta:** "many local drivers", usually with a wide hard shoulder (Komoot tip). — [Komoot 1070978](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/1070978)
- **Rentals:** no rental reviews were found. No first-hand forum reports on traffic were found.
- **Quote:** the north-side tarmac was "perfect" — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-mount-etna-sicily/)

## Crete
- **Drivers (views differ):**
  - Edelweiss advises avoiding main roads because "Greek drivers like to use most of the road". — [flyedelweiss.com](https://www.flyedelweiss.com/at/en/destinations/tips/road-cycling/crete.html), operator
  - A Chania rider in 2019: "I had no problems with drivers, dog or goats, but maybe that was just by chance" (single anecdote). — [Rick Steves forum "chania biking"](https://community.ricksteves.com/travel-forum/greece/chania-biking), 2019
- **Hazards:**
  - Goats that "charge down the hillside and across the road in front of you".
  - "Random big dogs chained up that get a bit rowdy" in villages.
  - Potholes on steep descents.
  - Some roads are "polished like glass" (motorcyclist).
  - A road covered in river pebbles after a flood.
  - — [road.cc forum "Cycling in Crete – what bike to take?"](https://road.cc/forums/topic/cycling-crete-what-bike-take-306571), [Singletrack](https://singletrackworld.com/forum/bike-forum/biking-in-crete/), [ADVrider](https://www.advrider.com/f/threads/motorcycle-rental-in-crete.1516778), dates unknown
- **Best months:** the operator recommends Feb–Apr and Sep–Nov. Highlight for one Chania rider: the Therisso gorge road. — sources above
- **Rentals:** no Chania road-bike rental reviews were found.

## Marrakech / High Atlas
- **Tizi n'Tichka is busy:** "There is lots of wind. And traffic. Construction lorries meet transport lorries meet tourist vans meet local buses." The new dual-carriageway near the top feels like "a city ring road". The writer recommends minor roads from Aït Benhaddou instead. — [Café du Cycliste "Tizi n'Tichka"](https://www.cafeducycliste.com/en/blogs/rouler/tizi-ntichka), brand blog, date unknown
- **Oukaïmeden:** the serious climb (to over 2,000 m), exposed and windy higher up. Tourist-area traffic builds later in the day, so start early. — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-the-high-atlas-from-marrakech-the-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/), guide
- **Dogs:** a *motorcyclist* counted "at least 50" dogs lying on the road descending from Tichka (single anecdote, not a cyclist). — [Motorcycle Mojo](https://motorcyclemojo.com/2023/04/marrakech-express-part-ii/), Apr 2023
- **Rentals:** no rental reviews were found.

## UAE (Jebel Jais)
- **Ride report:**
  - The rider went on a Tuesday morning in March to avoid Dubai traffic and valley heat.
  - Traffic was light apart from construction trucks, with goats on the road.
  - About 35 km at 4.1% from Ras Al Khaimah.
  - In 2017 the pavement ended near the top.
  - — [georgeron.com technical review](https://www.georgeron.com/2017/01/cycling-jebal-jais-technical-review.html), Jan 2017
- **Timing:**
  - Riders typically start between 05:30 and 06:30. — [visitjebeljais.com](https://visitjebeljais.com/blog/cycling-in-ras-al-khaimah-jebel-jais-trails-events)
  - "Not somewhere to reproduce the relaxed European cycling-holiday routine of… leaving the hotel at 11am". — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-dubai-desert-loops-jebel-jais-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- **Rentals:** no rental reviews were found; the search budget ran out before the Dubai rental search.

## Oman
- **Jebel Akhdar:** "The road hits 10% from the start". Only 4×4 cars are allowed up, and the descent needs care. — [Inner Ring reader account](https://inrng.com/?p=18556), date unknown
- **Heat:** a bikepacking report says the only safe riding window is from about 2 h after sunrise to before sunset. Some roads lack a shoulder. — [Topeak](https://www.topeak.com/global/en/news/52-BIKEPACKING-OMAN-–-THE-MUSCAT-LOOP), Jun 2018
- **Season:** "Best Time: Winter (Dec/Jan). Do not attempt in summer!" Short daylight hours meant racing the sunset. — [Bicycling SA](https://www.bicycling.co.za/travel/cycling-oman/), date unknown
- **Muscat coastal loop:** Mutrah, Qantab and Yiti have "very steep parts" but are scenic and fairly traffic-free outside the city (commenter). — [Inner Ring](https://inrng.com/?p=18556)
- **Rentals:** no rental reviews were found.

## Jordan

## Gran Canaria (reference)
- **Free Motion (Maspalomas):**
