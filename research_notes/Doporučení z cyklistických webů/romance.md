# Romance-language cycling sources: local recommendations (ES / CA / IT / PT / FR)

Research date: 2026-10-22. Method: WebSearch in Spanish, Catalan, Italian and Portuguese. WebFetch and curl could not reach any of the media sites (DNS errors or proxy refusals: ciclismoafondo.es, solobici.es, cicloturismo.comunitatvalenciana.com), so every bullet comes from search-result snippets, not from full articles. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Because of that, the **French-language sources were never searched** and the Portuguese coverage is thin (see Gaps).
Where a search in a local language returned English or German pages, the bullet says so ("non-local source"). Dates are given where the snippet showed one. "n.d." means no date was visible.

## Lanzarote

- **Verdict:** Ciclismo a Fondo covers the island mainly through its organised events. The Vuelta Ciclista a Lanzarote has 2 stages (115 km/1,700 m and 74 km/1,000 m) and passes through all 7 municipalities. The 2026 edition ran on 3–4 Oct. The route makes a ready-made 2-day template. — [Vuelta Ciclista a Lanzarote: todos los detalles del recorrido](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/vuelta-ciclista-lanzarote-todos-detalles-recorrido-ntv_295570_102.html), ES, 2025; [Principales marchas 2026](https://ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/principales-marchas-cicloturistas-2026_318847_102.html), ES, 2026
- **Must-ride segments (event timed sectors):**
  - Haría → Mirador del Río: 8.5 km at 4.5 %
  - Las Casitas → Femés: 5.3 km at 4 %
  - Timanfaya → Mancha Blanca: 12.7 km at 3 %

  — [Vuelta Ciclista a Lanzarote: todos los detalles](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/vuelta-ciclista-lanzarote-todos-detalles-recorrido-ntv_295570_102.html), ES, 2025
- **Climbs:**
  - Tabayesco (north) is the longest: about 10 km at 5 %.
  - Femés (south) is short but has a 21 % finish.
  - Suggested loop: El Golfo – La Geria vineyards – Playa Blanca – Femés.

  — [Ciclismo en las Islas Canarias: qué isla elegir](https://www.directferries.es/blog/vacaciones-bicicleta-islas-canarias), ES (ferry-company blog), n.d.; [cyclingspain.com](https://www.cyclingspain.com/?p=6303) (non-local source)
- **Wind tip:** Practise an aero position on the climb from Timanfaya, which is usually into a headwind. The road from Club La Santa towards Soo usually has a tailwind. Plan loops so the wind helps on the way home. — [220 Triathlon "Race insider"](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/220-triathlon-magazine/20180125/281668255391745), EN, 2018-01-25 (non-local source)
- **Base and rental:** Club La Santa (Tinajo) rents carbon Cannondale bikes and gives out route maps. Its routes range from 14 km to the 180 km IRONMAN course. It describes the roads as "well-kept roads with minimal traffic", with drivers who are considerate of cyclists. — [Club La Santa cycling](https://www.clublasanta.com/en/sports/cycling), EN, n.d. (operator page)
- **Economic note:** Club La Santa's sports events brought €19 million into Lanzarote's economy in 2024, which shows how established the island's sports-tourism infrastructure is. — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://ciclismoafondo.es/noticias/competiciones-club-santa-dejan-19-millones-euros-en-economia-lanzarote-en-2024_305174_102.html), ES, 2025
- **Warning:** Wind makes every route harder than its elevation profile suggests. — [directferries.es](https://www.directferries.es/blog/vacaciones-bicicleta-islas-canarias), ES, n.d.
- **Quote (ES):** "tiene un tramo cronometrado desde Haría hasta el Mirador del Río, de 8,5 km al 4,5 % de pendiente media". Gist: the timed sector of the island's own sportive runs from Haría up to Mirador del Río. — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/vuelta-ciclista-lanzarote-todos-detalles-recorrido-ntv_295570_102.html)

## Fuerteventura

- **Verdict:** Spanish media rate Lanzarote and Fuerteventura for open, quiet roads. Tenerife and Gran Canaria are rated for steeper, more demanding terrain. — [directferries.es](https://www.directferries.es/blog/vacaciones-bicicleta-islas-canarias), ES, n.d.
- **Key climb:** Antigua → Betancuria is about 5.5 km at about 6 %, with 14–15 % ramps. The descent towards Pájara is gentler. A detour Pájara → Ajuy (west-coast cliffs) adds about 18 km and 200 m of climbing. The hardest roads are Betancuria, Pájara and El Cardón. — [Bikeworld.pl "Gdzie na zgrupowanie? Fuerteventura"](https://portal.bikeworld.pl/artykul/turystyka/wyprawy_zagraniczne/13771/gdzie_na_zgrupowanie_fuerteventura), PL, n.d. (non-local source; no Spanish-language road guide surfaced)
- **Route:** The Gran Tarajal – Tuineje road over the mountains is described as well maintained, with a 13 % gradient. The south-west is the most mountainous part of the island. — [flyedelweiss / rove.me summary](https://flyedelweiss.com/ch/en/destinations/tips/road-cycling/canary-islands.html), EN, n.d. (non-local source)
- **Warnings:** Strong trade winds. Carry plenty of water. — [Bikeworld.pl](https://portal.bikeworld.pl/artykul/turystyka/wyprawy_zagraniczne/13771/gdzie_na_zgrupowanie_fuerteventura), PL, n.d.
- **Quote:** none found in Spanish.

## La Palma

- **Must-ride:** Santa Cruz de La Palma → Roque de los Muchachos (2,426 m) via the LP-1 and then the LP-4: about 41 km and about 2,500 m of climbing. Santa Cruz is the natural base (most hotels and restaurants). Descending the other side towards Hoya Grande (Garafía) and the LP-403 is described as a challenge in itself. — [Komoot ES "Rutas en bici en La Palma"](https://www.komoot.com/es-es/guide/820/rutas-en-bici-en-la-palma), ES, n.d.; [PJAMM](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/428.Roque%2520de%2520Los%2520Muchacho), EN
- **Other paved roads recommended:**
  - Carretera de las Mimbreras (LP-109)
  - the Traviesa de Puntagorda
  - the Fuencaliente coast road
  - Los Llanos de Aridane → Mirador de Los Brecitos: 34 km, about 1,350 m, "mostly paved"

  — [hellocanaryislands "Dare to cycle around La Palma"](https://www.hellocanaryislands.com/experiences/dare-to-cycle-around-la-palma/), official Canary tourism, n.d.; [Komoot ES](https://www.komoot.com/es-es/guide/820/rutas-en-bici-en-la-palma)
- **Warnings:**
  - There is no water on the Roque climb ([Climbfinder](https://climbfinder.com/us/climbs/roque-de-los-muchachos-santa-cruz-de-la-palma)).
  - The upper part is often in cloud in winter, and it can be very cold at the top in winter.
  - Some descent surfaces are poor (user comment, Nov 2024, Climbfinder).
  - On the Cumbre Vieja ridge, weather (fog, storms) changes fast, and fuel stations are rare on the Ruta de los Volcanes.
  - The Ruta de los Volcanes and Cumbre Vieja routes are MTB/forest tracks, not road routes.

  — [Komoot ES](https://www.komoot.com/es-es/guide/820/rutas-en-bici-en-la-palma); [senderosdelapalma.es](https://www.senderosdelapalma.es/en/?p=19777), n.d.
- **Quote:** none found in Spanish.

## Costa Blanca (Calpe / Dénia / Altea, Comunitat Valenciana)

- **Verdict:** In winter, WorldTour and continental teams cluster in Benidorm, Altea, Calpe and Dénia because of the weather. Inland Alicante roads are quiet at that time, except around Benidorm and on the Altea–Calpe stretch. — [Brújula Bike: Hotel Capnegret](https://en.brujulabike.com/hotel-capnegret-en/), ES/EN, n.d.; [Brújula Bike: Por qué España es el destino favorito de los equipos](https://en.brujulabike.com/spain-favourite-destination-professional-cycling-teams/), n.d.
- **Official view:** Calpe's town-hall brochure "Calpe destino cycling friendly" says local hotels offer bike storage, mechanical assistance and buffets aimed at cyclists. It names Cumbre del Sol, Coll de Rates, Tudons and Aitana as the local climbs. — [calpe.es PDF](https://calpe.es/sites/default/files/descubre_calpe_docs/2023-02/calpe_destino_cycling_friendly.pdf), ES, Feb 2023
- **Climbs:**
  - Coll de Rates: 6.6 km at 5.3 % from Parcent. It is "the climb most frequented by the pros in the area".
  - Vall d'Ebo: 9 km at 5.4 %, with ramps over 10 % in the first part. Pogačar holds the Strava KOM.
  - Puerto de Bernia: long, with a "trap" at the end.
  - Others: La Fustera, Alto de Tárbena, Confrides, Cumbre del Sol.

  — [Gobik "Cycling Getaway Chapter 6: Calpe"](https://gobik.com/en/blogs/gobik-stories/cycling-getaway-chapter-6-calpe), Spanish brand, n.d.; [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/ko-kr/guide/1774724); [Ciclismo a Fondo campus reports](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/finalizo-el-viii-campus-de-ciclismo-eduardo-chozas-activate-calpe-2013_36143_102.html), ES, 2013/2016/2024
- **Sample loops from Calpe:**
  - Coll de Rates + Castell de Castells: 91 km, 2,113 m (Ciclismo a Fondo campus, 2016)
  - Vall d'Ebo: 93 km, 1,710 m
  - Tudons: 111 km, 2,300 m (sportivebreaks)

  — [Ciclismo a Fondo 2016](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/marchas-cicloturistas/xi-campus-de-ciclismo-eduardo-chozas-activate-calpe-2016_39577_102.html); [Sportive Breaks](https://sportivebreaks.com/self-guided-road-cycling/calpe/) (non-local source)
- **Base choice:** Dénia has more variety of nearby climbs. Calpe gives access to Tudons and Confrides, but riding out of town means heavy traffic. — [Cycling Weekly](https://cyclingweekly.com/routes/overseas/cycling-denia-calpe-411675), EN (non-local source)
- **Hotels:** Hotel Capnegret in Altea is the WorldTour teams' "centro de operaciones". — [Brújula Bike](https://en.brujulabike.com/hotel-capnegret-en/), n.d.
- **Rentals:** Solybike (Calp) rents road bikes and runs tailor-made guided rides with a support van. It is listed on the official regional tourism site. — [comunitatvalenciana.com: Solybike](https://www.comunitatvalenciana.com/va/alacant-alicante/calp/empreses-de-servicis-complementaris/solybike), VA/ES, n.d.
- **Cafés and food:**
  - **Restaurante Coll de Rates** sits at the top of the pass and is the classic cyclists' stop. People mention the terrace views and the strudel (German–Spanish menu). It scores 7.7/10 from about 1,850 reviews, including reviews from 2026. — [Gastroranking](https://gastroranking.es/r/coll-de-rates_22056), ES, 2026
  - **D'Origin coffee** (Albir/Altea direction) is one of Spain's first micro-roasters, with its own farm in Panama. It is the stop on a 54 km Serra Gelada coffee ride. — [epicroadrides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/calpe-costa-blanca/coffee-ride-serra-gelada), EN (non-local source)
  - **Ca Joan** (Altea) is called one of the best restaurants in the Comunitat Valenciana, in Altea's rice and fish guide. — [Directo al Paladar gastroguía Altea](https://www.directoalpaladar.com/viajes/gastroguia-altea-donde-comer-mejores-arroces-pescados-pueblo-fotogenico-costa-blanca-alicantina), ES, n.d.
- **Warnings:**
  - The N-332 between Calpe and Albir is busy ([epicroadrides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/calpe-costa-blanca/coffee-ride-serra-gelada)).
  - Calpe police fine cyclists for running red lights: 10 pro riders were fined. — [Brújula Bike](https://en.brujulabike.com/10-professional-cyclists-fined-in-calpe-for-running-a-red-light/), n.d.
- **Quote (ES):** "Calpe se ha convertido en los últimos años en destino predilecto para equipos ciclistas de primer nivel para llevar a cabo su preparación durante los meses de invierno". Gist: in recent years Calpe has become the favourite winter-training base for top teams. — [calpe.es](https://calpe.es/sites/default/files/descubre_calpe_docs/2023-02/calpe_destino_cycling_friendly.pdf), Feb 2023

## Girona / Catalonia

- **Verdict:** Ciclismo a Fondo calls Girona the "new hub of world cycling", with a strong community feel and dozens of bars and restaurants specialised in cycling. — [¿Por qué Girona es el nuevo 'hub' del ciclismo mundial?](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/noticias/por-girona-es-nuevo-hub-ciclismo-mundial_265235_102.html), ES, about 2022
- **Classic climbs (Catalan sources):**
  - **Rocacorba:** about 10–13.8 km, averaging about 6.5–8 %. The hard part starts at the "Pujarnol" sign after an easy first 3 km. Girona → summit and back is about 80 km and 1,160 m. A local tip is to ride out via Medinyà on back roads towards Banyoles, use a stretch of the N-II with a wide shoulder, and ride it when the road is dry, not in high summer.
  - **Mare de Déu del Mont:** 18.5 km at 5 % from Cabanelles, or about 20 km at 5 % via medieval Besalú. Summit 1,093 m.
  - **Els Àngels.**
  - The big three (Rocacorba, Mare de Déu del Mont, Els Àngels) can be combined in one hard day.

  — [Eat Sleep Cycle (CA) Rocacorba](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/ct/classic-climbs-of-girona-rocacorba/), CA, n.d.; [Eat Sleep Cycle (CA) Mare de Déu del Mont](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/ct/classiques-escalades-de-girona-mare-de-deu-del-mont/), CA, n.d.; [Gobik Girona](https://gobik.com/en/blogs/gobik-stories/cycling-getaway-4-girona), n.d.
- **Coast:** The Sant Feliu de Guíxols – Tossa de Mar road is "considered by many cyclists the best road in Catalonia". Cap de Creus is also recommended. — [Costa Brava: territorio 100% ciclista](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/costa-brava-destino-100-ciclista_211131_102.html), ES, n.d.
- **Hotels:** Hotels Ciutat de Girona, CMC and Carlemany have bike stations and cyclist breakfasts. The regional tourism board gives a "bed & bike" seal to cyclist-friendly lodgings. — [Ciclismo a Fondo hub article](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/noticias/por-girona-es-nuevo-hub-ciclismo-mundial_265235_102.html), about 2022; [Costa Brava 100% ciclista](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/costa-brava-destino-100-ciclista_211131_102.html)
- **Cafés:**
  - **La Fábrica**, run by ex-pro Christian Meier and Amber Meier
  - **Federal Café**, run by ex-pro Rory Sutherland

  — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/noticias/por-girona-es-nuevo-hub-ciclismo-mundial_265235_102.html), about 2022. The article is about 4 years old, so check that both are still operating.
- **Events and timing:** Gran Fondo Lloret Costa Brava is in mid-April (18 Apr in the 2026 calendar). Sea Otter Europe is in Girona in September. — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/cicloturismo/costa-brava-destino-100-ciclista_211131_102.html)
- **Warnings:**
  - Setcases–Vallter (11.5 km, up to 13 %) is a ski-resort road, so it is not realistic in Jan–Apr. — [Solo Bici](https://solobici.es/en-bici-por-girona-rutas-de-ciclismo/), ES, n.d.
  - Some Komoot combinations (Figueres → Rocacorba + Mare de Déu del Mont, 124 km/2,330 m) include unpaved bits. — [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/guide/1594435/road-cycling-routes-around-llers)
- **Quote (ES):** "decenas de bares y restaurantes que se han especializado en este mundo ciclista". Gist: dozens of bars and restaurants in Girona now cater specifically to cyclists. — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/noticias/por-girona-es-nuevo-hub-ciclismo-mundial_265235_102.html)

## Andalusia (Málaga, Ronda, Grazalema, Almería)

- **Grazalema:**
  - Puerto de las Palomas is 12.7 km at 6.5 %. It decided the Vuelta a Andalucía stage that finished in Grazalema (2020). — [Ciclismo a Fondo (Fuglsang)](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/competicion/noticias-resultados-ciclismo/articulo/jakob-fuglsang-sigue-su-idilio-vuelta-andalucia-primer-lider), ES, 2020
  - An Andalusian pro (Juan Pedro López) says he prefers "el Boyar o las Palomas, en la Sierra de Grazalema" to the famous Alpine climbs. — [Ciclismo a Fondo "El relevo (II)"](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/competicion/entrevistas-ciclismo/relevo-ii-juan-pedro-lopez_160118_102.html), ES, 2016
- **Grazalema loops:**
  - Grazalema – Palomas – Zahara: 50–80 km and 1,500–2,200 m depending on variant. Avoid strong-wind days on the ridges. — [felicesvacaciones blog](https://blog.felicesvacaciones.es/que-rutas-hacer-en-grazalema), ES, n.d.
  - Grazalema–Zahara circular: 38 km, 1,000 m. — [Komoot ES Montejaque](https://www.komoot.com/es-es/guide/1593586/rutas-en-bici-en-montejaque)
- **Local operator:** Andalucian Cycling Experience in Montecorto (Ronda) has been running since 2005. It is the oldest road and MTB operator in the Serranía de Ronda, Grazalema and Sierra de las Nieves, and offers guided and self-guided trips. — [andalucia.org](https://andalucia.org/en/ronda-sport-activities-andalucian-cycling-experience), official tourism, n.d.
- **Ronda:** Puerto del Viento (Ronda → El Burgo, about 13.3 km) is a popular climb. — [Komoot "cycling around Ronda"](https://www.komoot.com/guide/1623302/cycling-around-ronda)
- **Málaga:**
  - Puerto del León / Fuente de la Reina through the Montes de Málaga natural park: about 16 km at 5 % to about 900 m. Good asphalt and calm traffic. A 56 km loop has 1,350 m of climbing.
  - Return via Olías – El Palo – Pedregalejo.
  - Málaga → Cómpeta (Axarquía, "la Toscana malagueña"): 106 km.

  — [Guía Repsol "Rutas en bicicleta por la provincia de Málaga"](https://www.guiarepsol.com/es/viajar/vamos-de-excursion/rutas-bicicleta-malaga-provincia/), ES, n.d.; [Bikemap](https://www.bikemap.net/es/c/336603/)
- **Almería:**
  - Alto de Velefique: 13.1 km at 7.2 %.
  - Calar Alto: 16.9 km at 5.6 % to 2,168 m.
  - Both were used on Vuelta 2026 stage 12 (3 Sep 2026: 166 km, about 4,530 m of climbing).
  - Calar Alto at 2,168 m may be cold or snowy in mid-winter (inference; no source checked winter access).

  — [Brújula Bike](https://en.brujulabike.com/calar-alto-puts-enric-mas-roglic-and-gall-to-the-test-in-vuelta-2026-stage-12/), 2026
- **Quote (ES):** "el Boyar o las Palomas, en la Sierra de Grazalema". Gist: a local pro's favourite climbs are in Grazalema, not the Alps. — [Ciclismo a Fondo](https://www.ciclismoafondo.es/competicion/entrevistas-ciclismo/relevo-ii-juan-pedro-lopez_160118_102.html), 2016

## Sicily (Etna, Nebrodi)

- **Verdict / pro use:** Pro Simone Velasco was on his 5th Etna camp, training with Fortunato and Sbaragli. Their days included over 6.5 h with more than 4,000 m of climbing, the full loop of the volcano (about 180 km) was a goal, and the final climb was always done on the bike. On long rides he refuels with **arancini**, with 1–2 short stops. — [bici.PRO "Chilometri, salite, arancini: Velasco e i racconti dall'Etna"](https://bici.pro/focus/storie/chilometri-salite-arancini-velasco-racconti-etna/), IT, 2024-01-28
- **Climbs:**
  - Nicolosi → Rifugio Sapienza: 17 km, 1,073 m, 6.3 %, smooth asphalt. Contador won the Giro stage here in 2011.
  - Linguaglossa → Piano Provenzana: 18.2 km, 1,235 m, 6.8 %. Giro 2020 finish.
  - Ragalna → Piano Vetore: 14 km. Giro 2018.
  - East side to Contrada Giuliana: 18 km. Giro di Sicilia 2022.
  - The **Parco Ciclistico dell'Etna** (opened 2019 in Nicolosi) signposts 6 climbs with information panels.

  — [Tuttobiciweb Giro 2020 Etna](https://www.tuttobiciweb.it/article/2020/10/05/1601826854/giro-d-italia-tappa-dell-etna-arrivo-in-salita-arrivo-sull-etna), IT, 2020-10-05; [Tuttobiciweb Parco Ciclistico](https://www.tuttobiciweb.it/article/2019/04/02/1554223884/parco-ciclistico-etna-inaugurato-a-nicolisi-alla-vigilia-del-giro-di-sicilia), IT, 2019-04-02; [inrng Roads to Ride](https://inrng.com/?p=31088) (non-local source)
- **Loops:**
  - Full Etna loop: 150–180 km depending on variant. — [Cicloturismo.it](https://www.cicloturismo.it/letna-in-bicicletta-centocinquanta-chilometri-intorno-al-vulcano-attivo-piu-alto-deuropa/), IT, n.d.
  - Bronte – Randazzo: 45 km, 780 m, with a 22 % section. — [Bikeitalia Bronte](https://www.bikeitalia.it/sicilia-in-bicicletta-viaggio-alla-scoperta-di-bronte-e-del-parco-delletna/), IT, n.d.
  - Adrano south-side loop: 38 km, 1,210 m. — [Komoot IT](https://www.komoot.com/it-it/guide/163658/fantastici-percorsi-per-bici-da-corsa-nei-dintorni-del-parco-dell-etna)
- **Base:** "Nicolosi è la migliore base per le salite all'Etna da Sud." — [Wikivoyage IT Nicolosi](https://it.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Nicolosi), IT, n.d.
- **Nebrodi:**
  - Portella Femmina Morta (1,524 m) is the highest pass in Sicily and has been used by the Giro.
  - A loop from Troina via the Ancipa dam, San Teodoro and Cesarò has more than 800 m of climbing.

  — [Il Sole 24 Ore "In sella"](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/uno-strappo-vista-nebrodi-AE9MRh9E), IT, n.d.
- **Food and wine (Gambero Rosso, north side of Etna):**
  - **San Giorgio e il Drago** (Randazzo): roast lamb with Nerello reduction; closed Tuesdays.
  - **Boccaperta** (Linguaglossa).
  - **Monte Conca** (Piano Provenzana).
  - **Shalai** (Linguaglossa): chef Giovanni Santoro.
  - **Cave Ox** and **Terra Mia** (Solicchiata).
  - **Etna Quota Mille**, near Randazzo, in a restored palmento (from italia.it).

  — [Gambero Rosso "lato più nascosto dell'Etna"](https://www.gamberorosso.it/ristoranti/dove-mangiare-e-cosa-vedere-nel-lato-piu-nascosto-etna-migliori-ristoranti), IT, n.d.; [Gambero Rosso International](https://www.gamberorossointernational.com/?p=516900); [italia.it](https://www.italia.it/it/sicilia/cosa-fare/itinerario-tra-etna-taormina-e-dintorni)
- **Warnings:**
  - Strong wind and fast weather changes at altitude.
  - The Zafferana road was found closed by snow at 800 m in Feb 2023.
  - Fill bottles in Nicolosi, because there is no water on the lava fields.
  - Very steep section after Poggiofelice (Zafferana side).

  — [camperonline forum](https://www.camperonline.it/forum/viaggiare/viaggi-in-italia/sicilia-etna/220264), IT; [cycleblaze](https://www.cycleblaze.com/journals/sicilia/day-ride-to-rifugio-sapienza), EN, 2023-04-12
- **Best months:** Winter suits coastal touring ("le temperature non scendono mai sotto zero"). Guides favour spring and autumn for the mountains. — [Wikivoyage IT Cicloturismo in Sicilia](https://it.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Cicloturismo_in_Sicilia)

## Tuscany (Val d'Orcia, Chianti, Strade Bianche, L'Eroica)

- **L'Eroica permanent route:**
  - About 208–209 km and 3,300–4,000 m (sources disagree), with signposting, through the Chianti, Crete Senesi and Val d'Orcia.
  - Rideable all year from any town on the route. Partner refreshment stops are open year-round.
  - "la Gravel è la bici ideale" (gravel is the ideal bike).
  - Stage suggestions: Gaiole – Montalcino (87 km, 1,410 m), or Gaiole – Buonconvento (66 km) then Buonconvento – Asciano (78 km).

  — [Visit Tuscany "Eroica in bicicletta"](https://www.visittuscany.com/it/itinerari-bike/eroica-in-bicicletta/), IT, n.d.; [siviaggia](https://siviaggia.it/vacanze-natura/percorso-permanente-eroica/247820/), IT, n.d.
- **Terra Eroica:** A network of 288 km and 5,400 m in total. The map and stamp kit costs €10 at "Bike Point" partners. It includes hospitality, restaurant and agri-food partners. — [bici.PRO "Terra Eroica, i percorsi permanenti"](https://bici.pro/turismo/terra-eroica-percorsi-permanenti-gusto-impresa/), IT, about 2022
- **Hardest sector:** The Monte Sante Marie gravel sector (from the Strade Bianche race) is "molto impegnativa". — [Visit Tuscany](https://www.visittuscany.com/en/itineraries-bike/eroica-by-bike/)
- **Val d'Orcia loops:**
  - Pienza – San Quirico – Bagno Vignoni: about 40 km, 750 m, mixed surface.
  - Anello del Brunello (Montalcino – Sant'Antimo – Castelnuovo dell'Abate): about 27 km, 600 m. The Sant'Angelo in Colle → Passo del Lume Spento climb is the hard part.

  — [thewom travel "Val d'Orcia in bici"](https://travel.thewom.it/italia/toscana/val-d-orcia-in-bici.html), IT, n.d.
- **Chianti (paved):** The Grand Tour del Chianti fiorentino goes over the Passo del Sugame (6.4 km, 301 m) and the Passo del Morellino (the highest point in the Chianti), then Radda and back to Greve via Panzano. — [Visit Tuscany "Grand Tour del Chianti fiorentino"](https://www.visittuscany.com/it/itinerari-bike/grand-tour-del-chianti-fiorentino/), IT, n.d.
- **Tips:** Start early (heat, in summer). Services between villages are limited, so carry food and water. Montalcino → Pieve a Salti is 34 km with many tough rollers and views of Amiata. — [Bikeitalia "Un viaggio sulle strade dell'Eroica"](https://www.bikeitalia.it/2015/10/13/un-viaggio-in-bicicletta-sulle-strade-delleroica/), IT, 2015-10-13
- **Warning:** Gravel surfaces change with rain. On a road bike, use wide tyres or choose the paved variants. — [siviaggia](https://siviaggia.it/vacanze-natura/percorso-permanente-eroica/247820/); [Visit Tuscany](https://www.visittuscany.com/it/itinerari-bike/eroica-in-bicicletta/)
- **Food:** The Gambero Rosso search found no Chianti trattoria names in the snippets. That gap is not filled.
- **Quote (IT):** "fruibile con qualsiasi tipo di bici (ma la Gravel è la bici ideale)". Gist: any bike will do on the Eroica route, but gravel is best. — [Visit Tuscany](https://www.visittuscany.com/it/itinerari-bike/eroica-in-bicicletta/)

## Algarve

- **Volta ao Algarve climbs (Portuguese press):**
  - **Alto da Fóia (Monchique):** the highest point in the Algarve. 7.5 km at 6 % (2025). The 2026 race used a harder north-side variant: 8.4 km, with a steeper section about 3.5 km from the top.
  - **Pomba:** 3.6 km at 8.2 %, about 6 km before the foot of the Fóia.
  - **Alto do Malhão (Loulé):** decided the race in 2022 (Evenepoel) and 2024. It was climbed twice in 2025. The 2026 race was decided by a time trial from Salir (19.6 km, 17 of them flat) finishing on the Malhão. The climb is about 2.6 km at 9.2 %.

  — [Record "Alto do Malhão volta a definir o vencedor"](https://www.record.pt/modalidades/ciclismo/detalhe/alto-do-malhao-volta-a-definir-o-vencedor-da-volta-ao-algarve), PT, n.d.; [Renascença/Bola Branca "subida mais dura à Fóia"](https://rr.pt/bola-branca/noticia/modalidades/2026/02/17/volta-ao-algarve-arranca-com-nova-organizacao-e-uma-subida-mais-dura-a-foia-o-que-a-torna-tao-atrativa/459792/), PT, 2026-02-17; [Flashscore PT](https://www.flashscore.pt/noticias/ciclismo-volta-ao-algarve-com-dupla-subida-ao-malhao-e-contrarrelogio-no-ultimo-dia/CjMq74rC/), PT, 2025; [Barlavento](https://www.barlavento.pt/?p=362447), PT, n.d.
- **Timing:** The Volta ao Algarve runs mid-to-late February (rr.pt article of 17 Feb 2026 reports the 2026 edition "arranca", i.e. starting). Riding the climbs the week after the race is a natural plan (inference).
