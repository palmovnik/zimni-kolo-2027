# What English-language cycling media say about winter/spring road-cycling destinations

Compiled 22 Oct 2026 for a 4–5 night road-cycling trip in Jan–Apr/May 2027 (group of 5–6 Czech riders, rental bikes, food lovers).

**How this was researched:** WebFetch and curl could not reach any site from this environment (DNS failure or proxy 403). Every bullet therefore comes from **web search result snippets and summaries**, not from reading full articles. The search budget also ran out partway through, before searches on hotels, bike hire and restaurants for most locations. Details such as gradients may differ slightly from the full articles. Where a summary combined several sources, a bullet cites the most specific URL that the claim came from. Article dates are often unknown. When the search index gave only an age, it is converted to an approximate date. Nothing below is invented. If a field has no bullets, the sources returned nothing for it.

## Lanzarote

**Verdict and best months**
- Rated for "excellent temperatures in December–March" — [Winter cycling holidays: best places to cycle November to March](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/winter-cycling-holidays/), Epic Road Rides, date unknown (≤ ~2 years old)
- The island's subtropical location keeps it warm, with no snow risk — [Best cycling holidays: our guide on where to go…](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/cycling-holiday-365786), Cycling Weekly, ~2023
- Listed by Rouleur among Spain's year-round cycling hotspots, together with Girona, Mallorca, Calpe and Tenerife — [The best cycling holidays: top locations…](https://www.rouleur.cc/blogs/rouleur-explore/the-best-places-to-go-cycling-around-the-world), Rouleur, date unknown

**Routes and climbs**
- A northern loop of 116 km with ~1,750 m of climbing takes in Mirador del Río, which has "magnificent views of the whole island" — [Cyclist's guide to cycling in Lanzarote](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-spain/lanzarote/), Epic Road Rides, date unknown
- The Timanfaya/volcano tour from Playa Blanca is ~114–117 km with 1,300–1,400 m of climbing, "a tough workout" — [ProCyclingUK: Cycling in Lanzarote](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-lanzarote-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/), date unknown
- Tabayesco has "a leg-firing ascent and hairpin bends". Forum riders call the climb past it a Cat 1 ascent to the island's highest accessible road — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-lanzarote-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/) and [BikeRadar forum: Lanzarote Road Cycling](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12927525/lanzarote-road-cycling), forum ~2010–2018
- Órzola → Mirador del Río is 8.6 km with up to 470 m of gain, "one of the most demanding" routes — [La Voz de Lanzarote: best road cycling routes](https://www.lavozdelanzarote.com/en/things-to-do-in-lanzarote/the-best-road-biking-routes-in-lanzarote_213715_102.html), date unknown
- Playa Blanca → Femés is "a tough climb but great fun", one of a rider's two best rides on the island — [CycleChat: Cycling in Lanzarote](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cycling-in-lanzarote.202270/latest), forum, date unknown
- A route from Puerto del Carmen runs through the La Geria vineyards (~350 m) towards El Golfo and Femés — [Vintage Travel: Top cycle routes on Lanzarote](https://www.vintagetravel.co.uk/blog/?p=5510), date unknown
- Cyclist's travel site offers a self-guided 180 km loop along the island's spine, or an 80 km version, through Timanfaya National Park — [Cyclist travel: Cycling Lanzarote](https://travel.cyclist.co.uk/cycling-holidays/bikecation/cycling-lanzarote/), date unknown

**Bases, hotels and bike hire**
- Bases: Costa Teguise or Puerto del Carmen for bike-focused trips, Playa Blanca for upscale stays with some cycling, Club La Santa for triathletes — [8 best bike hotels on Lanzarote](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/best-bike-hotels-lanzarote/), Epic Road Rides, ~mid-2025
- Hotels named in that article:
  - Lanzarote Prime Sports by Vitalclass (Costa Teguise), "perfect as cycling accommodation", a strong pick for small groups
  - Sands Beach Active Resort (Costa Teguise)
  - Hotel Costa Calero (Puerto Calero), which has a cycling tour company in its basement plus bike rental and storage
  - Secrets Lanzarote (Puerto Calero), 5-star and adults-only, with bike storage
  - Dreams Lanzarote Playa Dorada and MYND Yaiza (Playa Blanca)
  - Sandos Papagayo
  - Club La Santa

  Source for the whole list: [8 best bike hotels on Lanzarote](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/best-bike-hotels-lanzarote/), ~mid-2025
- Bike hire is available in Costa Teguise, Puerto del Carmen, Playa Blanca and La Santa. Named shops are Planet Bikes and Bike Sensations — [8 best bike hotels on Lanzarote](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/best-bike-hotels-lanzarote/)
- Bike Sensations has 3 shops in Puerto del Carmen and 1 in Playa Blanca and offers "a wide range of road bikes". It also provides roadside assistance and guided road rides of 60–70 km — [Gazette Life: where to rent a bike in Puerto del Carmen](https://gazettelife.com/news/where-to-rent-a-bike-in-puerto-del-carmen/), Jul 2022 (local English-language paper, not cycling media)
- Club La Santa runs guided group rides that build up to a 120 km "Endurance Road Bike Tour" over the island's toughest climbs — [Cyclist (sponsored): Club La Santa](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/sponsored/club-la-santa-the-best-training-playground-for-cyclists), ~2024 (**sponsored content**)

**Warnings**
- **Wind** is the main caveat: "can be ferocious on the north coast near Club La Santa", and locals say it is particularly strong from January to April — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-lanzarote-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- A forum rider reports "50kph+ head and side winds" and suggests basing in the south so rides finish with a tailwind — [BikeRadar forum](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/12927525/lanzarote-road-cycling), old
- The prevailing wind is northeasterly. One trip had a steady 20 mph northerly, and "the only way out of PdC [Puerto del Carmen] is by climbing in a northerly direction" — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cycling-in-lanzarote.202270/latest)

**Characteristic quote:** "risk that the wind will utterly destroy the enjoyment" — CycleChat forum rider ([link](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/cycling-in-lanzarote.202270/latest))

## Fuerteventura

**Verdict and best months**
- Cyclist's Big Ride "Ride like the wind in Fuerteventura" presents it as a less-crowded cycling destination. The ride passes through the mountains of Parque Rural de Betancuria — [Cyclist Big Ride](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/fuerteventura-gran-canaria-cycling), ~Nov 2024
- The riding is exposed rolling roads, steady inland climbs and long wind-affected stretches. The climbing is "generally steady rather than extreme" — [ProCyclingUK: Cycling in Fuerteventura](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/), ~2025–26

**Routes and climbs**
- The island's official long-distance road route is ~146 km with ~1,790 m of climbing. Betancuria has "sustained climbing, technical descents" — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- Named climbs with viewpoints at the top:
  - Mirador Astronómico de Sicasumbre, on a 95 km loop with ~1,295 m of climbing
  - Mirador de las Peñitas and Mirador de Morro Velosa, on a 108 km loop with ~1,610 m via Betancuria

  Source: [Sportive Breaks: Playitas Resort](https://sportivebreaks.com/uncategorized/playitas-resort/), date unknown
- A Cofete loop crosses the southern tip, with tough short climbs and long coastal sections — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- "There aren't any really high climbs on the island – the climb to Betancuria is quite nice though" — [BikeRadar forum: Fuerteventura](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/13060669/fuerteventura), date unknown

**Bases and hotels**
- Corralejo (north) has the best riding from the door. Caleta de Fuste is the central option. Costa Calma and Morro Jable (south) are quieter but far from varied terrain — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- Playitas Resort (Las Playitas) is the sports-resort option sold by Sportive Breaks — [Sportive Breaks](https://sportivebreaks.com/uncategorized/playitas-resort/)

**Warnings**
- Trade wind "can turn an apparently straightforward 90km ride into a far harder day than the elevation profile suggests". Plan loops so the exposed leg comes first, on the way out — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- **Conflict on timing:** ProCyclingUK says the trades are strongest in spring and summer. A forum poster says the wind blows from the NE "most of the winter". Another rider failed to do the Betancuria climb on three visits because of wind — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/), [BikeRadar forum](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19864952/)
- Much of the best riding is inland, where the wind is weaker. Few places sell water inland, so carry plenty. Watch the surfaces on descents — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/), [BikeRadar forum](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19864952/)

**Characteristic quote:** Cyclist's headline "Ride like the wind" says it all ([link](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/fuerteventura-gran-canaria-cycling))

## La Palma

**Verdict**
- Epic Road Rides says the Roque de los Muchachos climb from sea level "beats its neighbour El Teide". It also notes that on La Palma hiking, and even mountain biking, get more attention than road cycling — [Cycling the Canary Islands: which island is best for you?](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-canary-islands/), date unknown

**Routes and climbs**
- **Roque de los Muchachos from Santa Cruz de La Palma (Simon Warren):** 41.5 km, summit 2,426 m, average 5.7%, maximum 15%. The road reaches the cloud line after ~1,500 m of climbing. At km 35 the Mirador de los Andenes looks over the Caldera de Taburiente — [BikeRadar: best cycling climbs in Spain, according to Simon Warren](https://www.bikeradar.com/features/routes-and-rides/best-cycling-climbs-in-spain), date unknown
- West side from Mirador El Serradero: segments of 11.75 km at 7%, then 3.3 km at 6.6%, then 12.2 km at 8.3%. A visitor-centre fee was €15 for non-residents (2022) — [PJAMM: Roque de los Muchachos west](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/5321.Roque-de-los-Muchachos-west)
- The official tourist route is 127 km north → south and includes a hard 15 km climb to the observatory — [Hello Canary Islands: cycling route La Palma](https://www.hellocanaryislands.com/cycling/la-palma/cycling-route-la-palma) (tourism board)

**Warnings**
- Ramps up to 12% on the east side. "Fog, clouds and snow" can make the road dangerous, and it is much colder at the top than on the coast — [PJAMM east](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/428.Roque%20de%20Los%20Muchacho)

**No results for:** a base town, hotels, bike hire or food. A search for these was cut off by the search limit.

## Costa Blanca (Calpe / Dénia)

**Verdict and best months**
- One of Cyclist's top 5 winter-sun picks: "up until late February, almost every pro team will be descending on this small coastal holiday town" — [Cyclist: Top five winter sun holidays](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/our-top-last-minute-cycling-winter-escapes), date unknown
- "March and April are the absolute peak for cycling in Calpe – warmer days, longer light and buzzing cycling activity" — [Spring cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/spring-cycling-holidays/), Epic Road Rides, date unknown
- Around 20 °C on average from January to March. Winter and spring are the most popular riding seasons in Calpe — [eatsleepcycle: Calpe winter cycling holiday](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/calpe-winter-cycling-holiday/), date unknown
- "325 sunny days per year". Pro teams named as visitors: Dimension Data, INEOS, Trek-Segafredo and Sunweb — [AR Hotels: Legendary cycling in Calpe](https://www.ar-hotels.com/en/blog/legendary-cycling-in-calpe.html) (hotel marketing)

**Routes and climbs**
- **Coll de Rates:** ~6–6.5 km at ~5%. It "consistently ranks in the top ten of the most ridden mountains every year" — [eatsleepcycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/calpe-winter-cycling-holiday/), [AR Hotels](https://www.ar-hotels.com/en/blog/legendary-cycling-in-calpe.html)
- **Val d'Ebo:** category 2, 8 km at 6%. Other routes named are Cumbre del Sol and the Calpe–Parcent–Guadalest loop — [eatsleepcycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/calpe-winter-cycling-holiday/), [AR Hotels](https://www.ar-hotels.com/en/blog/legendary-cycling-in-calpe.html)

**Cafés**
- Blanca Bikes café sits "right on the route to/from Coll de Rates" and is praised for its terrace and coffee. The café at the summit is seasonal, so check its winter opening — [eatsleepcycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/calpe-winter-cycling-holiday/) and/or [AR Hotels](https://www.ar-hotels.com/en/blog/legendary-cycling-in-calpe.html) (the summary did not say which source each claim came from)

**Warnings**
- Pro-team season runs to late February, so expect busy roads on the classic climbs — [Cyclist](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/our-top-last-minute-cycling-winter-escapes)

**No results for:** Dénia specifically, Calpe restaurants or bike hire. A search for these was cut off by the search limit.

## Girona

**Verdict**
- One of Cyclist's top 5 winter-sun picks — [Cyclist: Top five winter sun holidays](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/our-top-last-minute-cycling-winter-escapes)
- Also on Rouleur's list of year-round Spanish hotspots — [Rouleur](https://www.rouleur.cc/blogs/rouleur-explore/the-best-places-to-go-cycling-around-the-world)

**Routes and climbs**
- **Els Àngels:** "the most iconic climb in Girona", with a monastery at the top. It averages just 3.5%. Roadman calls it the ideal first ride: it "gets you above the plains without destroying your legs" — [eatsleepcycle: Cyclist's guide Girona](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/cyclists-guide-girona), [Roadman: Girona cycling guide](https://roadmancycling.com/blog/girona-cycling-guide)
- **Rocacorba:** "Girona's most famous climbing test segment", 11.8 km at 6.4% with a 12% maximum. Rides that include it run 60–80 km — [ctyeh: Girona Cycling Paradise](https://ctyeh.com/articles/1499?lang=en), [Laka: Girona travel guide](https://laka.co/gb/blog/road-bike/laka-rides-in-spain-the-ultimate-travel-guide-to-the-lanes-of-girona)
- **Mare de Déu del Mont:** a harder full-day option, with "no cafe at the summit, just a monastery, some goats, and silence" — [Roadman](https://roadmancycling.com/blog/girona-cycling-guide)

**Cafés and food**
- **La Fàbrica:** "the unofficial headquarters of cycling in Girona". "While Girona is now full of 'cycling friendly' coffee shops, La Fábrica was the first." Sources disagree on who founded it (Christian Meier vs "Australian riders") — [Roadman](https://roadmancycling.com/blog/girona-cycling-guide), [eatsleepcycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/cyclists-guide-girona)
- **Federal Café** for a post-ride sit-down meal: "the brunch menu is outstanding" — [Roadman](https://roadmancycling.com/blog/girona-cycling-guide)
- La Comuna, a meeting point before rides, and Espresso Mafia in the city centre — [eatsleepcycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/cyclists-guide-girona) / [Gobik: Cycling Getaway Girona](https://gobik.com/en/blogs/gobik-stories/cycling-getaway-4-girona)

**No results for:** warnings about Girona in winter (cold mornings, rain). A search for these was cut off by the search limit.

## Andalusia (Málaga / Ronda / Marbella / Almería)

**Ronda**
- Ronda is "surrounded by stunning mountain ranges, boasting a wealth of fantastic loop rides". **Puerto de las Palomas** is "one of the most iconic cycling passes in Andalucia". The day ride Ronda → Palomas → Grazalema is ~86 km with ~1,650 m of climbing — [Skedaddle: Roads of Ronda](https://www.skedaddle.com/uk/classicroad/holiday/Road_Cycling/Spain_-_Roads_of_Ronda_-_Self-Guided_Road_Cycling_Holiday/485/view.rails) (tour operator)
- Skedaddle runs its guided Ronda tour in **March and November**, "an excellent option for riders looking for a little early or late season cycling in the sunshine" — [Skedaddle guided](https://www.skedaddle.com/us/classicroad/holiday/Road_Cycling/Spain_-_Roads_of_Ronda_-_Guided_Road_Cycling_Holiday/484/view.rails)
- Cycling Spain's white-villages tour includes Puerto del Boyar, Zahara and Puerto de las Palomas, priced at €625 per person in a double room with breakfast (2026 price) — [Cycling Spain: Ronda & the white villages](https://www.cyclingspain.com/holidays/road-cycling-ronda-and-the-white-villages)

**Almería and the Sierra Nevada**
- Costa Almería's winter temperatures average "16 to 22 degrees celsius" — [Winter cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/winter-cycling-holidays/), Epic Road Rides
- Cyclist has a Big Ride on Pico del Veleta (Sierra Nevada, Granada), but it is a summer, high-altitude ride and not relevant for winter — [Cyclist Big Ride: Pico del Veleta](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/2664/big-ride-into-thin-air-on-the-pico-del-veleta)

## Cyprus

**Verdict and best months**
- "Cyprus is a year-round cycling destination, but November through to April is particularly good – mild, dry, and perfect for climbing" — [Spring cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/spring-cycling-holidays/), Epic Road Rides
- Paphos is mild from December to March, but rain is more likely and mountain weather less predictable. Snow is possible in the Troodos from January to March — [Cyprus – the ultimate guide for road cyclists](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-cyprus/), Epic Road Rides
- Two Cycling UK magazine features cover Cyprus off-season: "Great Rides: Cyprus in the off season" (a February circuit that crossed the Troodos and found it "very cold" with snow on the road near the summit) and "Great Rides: Springtime in Cyprus" — [Cycling UK: off season](https://www.cyclinguk.org/cycle/great-rides-cyprus-season), [Cycling UK: springtime](https://www.cyclinguk.org/cycle-magazine/great-rides-springtime-cyprus)

**Routes and climbs**
- Troodos is the main climbing area. Mount Olympus (1,952 m) is the highest point — [Epic Road Rides Cyprus](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-cyprus/)
- Rides from Paphos:
  - Mount Olympus ride: just over 150 km and over 3,000 m of climbing. The longest climb is 58.6 km at 3.2% average, with forest ramps up to ~13%
  - Paphos Forest loop: ~130 km and nearly 3,000 m
  - Easy coastal ride Paphos → Pissouri Bay via the Rock of Aphrodite: 72 km and ~750 m

  Sources: [Cycling Mount Olympus](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-cyprus/cycling-mount-olympus-cyprus/), [Paphos Forest route](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-cyprus/cyprus-cycling-route-paphos-forest/), [Cycling Paphos](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-cyprus/paphos/)
- From Limassol, a Troodos route runs 117 km with 2,660 m of climbing via the Lofou area. February to May is when conditions on it are usually best — [Cycling Limassol](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-cyprus/limassol/)

**Gear and tips**
- Pack a gilet for wind and a long-sleeve jacket and gloves for the mountains. On the steepest Troodos roads, use a compact chainset with an 11-28 cassette — [Cyprus cycling holidays: 18 things you must know](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cyprus-cycling-holidays-tips/)

**Bases:** Paphos and Limassol both have dedicated Epic Road Rides pages covering routes and bike shops. The search snippets did not name specific shops.

## Madeira

**Verdict**
- Madeira is best suited to experienced climbers, with few flat sections — [Cycling in Portugal: the inside guide](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-portugal-overview/), Epic Road Rides
- Cycling Weekly asks "Is Madeira the ultimate adventure destination?" and also covers Porto Santo (drier, ~1,000 m of climbing in a 40 km loop, plus a flat, fast coastal road on the south side) — [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/routes/overseas/madeira-ultimate-adventure-destination-399297), older article

**Routes and climbs**
- **Pico do Arieiro:** the first 2.6 km average ~20% — [Epic Road Rides Portugal](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-portugal-overview/)
- PJAMM calls it the hardest climb in Portugal, at a 9.8% average. A gentler variant from central Funchal averages ~8.3% — [PJAMM: Pico Arieiro](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/1071.Eira-Do-Serrado---Easier-Route)
- Rouleur Travel runs a 4-day "Madeira Bike & Hike" from Funchal: 54–76 km and 1,400–2,720 m of climbing per day — [Rouleur travel: Madeira Bike & Hike](https://www.rouleur.cc/en-us/pages/rouleur-travel-madeira-bike-hike)

**Warnings**
- Roads are well surfaced and drivers considerate, but there are few interesting road routes. The coast is built up and busy with tourist traffic — [Cycling UK forum: Has anyone road cycled Madeira?](https://forum.cyclinguk.org/viewtopic.php?t=153996)
- Road cycling is uncommon on the island and some old coast roads are closed — [BikeRadar forum: Cycle Hire in Madeira](https://forum.bikeradar.com/discussion/comment/19911345)
- Riders report spending most of their time in first or second gear — (same forum threads)

## Algarve

**Verdict and best months**
- One of Cyclist's top 5 winter-sun picks, but "wind is a huge factor here so be prepared to ride through multiple headwinds" — [Cyclist: Top five winter sun holidays](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/our-top-last-minute-cycling-winter-escapes)
- Cyclist also ran "Winter sun in Portugal's Algarve: cycling's hidden paradise" — [Cyclist](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/in-depth/winter-sun-in-portugals-algarve-cyclings-hidden-paradise), date unknown
- Winter highs can reach about 20 °C — [Winter cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/winter-cycling-holidays/)
- Summer is peak season with busy roads, so spring, autumn or winter is recommended — [eatsleepcycle: Guide to cycling in the Algarve](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/us/guide-to-cycling-in-the-algarve/)

**Routes and climbs**
- **Fóia (Monchique):** 7.7 km, 456 m of gain, 5.9% average. It features in the Volta ao Algarve, and traffic in the Serra de Monchique "can drop considerably" — [Cycling in the Algarve](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-portugal/algarve/), Epic Road Rides
- **Serra de Tavira** (north of Tavira) is "vastly more hilly and home to plenty of car free roads". Alcaria do Cume is 10.6 km at 3% and is "more about the views" — [Epic Road Rides Algarve](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-portugal/algarve/)
- Bases: Salgados, Tavira and Lagos. Monchique is 20–30 km from Lagos — [Cycling Lagos: 9 best routes](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/lagos-cycling-routes-algarve-portugal/)
- BikeRadar feature: "Two wheels in the Algarve: Roman cobbles, perfect scenery and the deep blue Atlantic" — [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/features/routes-and-rides/two-wheels-in-the-algarve-roman-cobbles-perfect-scenery-and-the-deep-blue-atlantic), ~2019

## Sicily (Etna)

**Verdict and best months**
- April, May, September and October offer the best balance. Winter can bring snow high on the volcano — [Cycling Etna, Sicily – what you need to know](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-mount-etna-sicily/), Epic Road Rides, and [ProCyclingUK: Cycling in Sicily – the Etna region](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-sicily-the-etna-region-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/)

**Routes and climbs**
- **South side:** Nicolosi → Rifugio Sapienza (1,892 m), ~18 km at ~7% — same sources
- **North side:** Linguaglossa → Piano Provenzana (~1,800 m), ~18 km at nearly 7%. You cannot ride to the summit — same sources
- Etna was a Giro d'Italia 2022 summit finish (stage 4) — [Rouleur: Giro 2022 stage 4 preview](https://www.rouleur.cc/blogs/the-rouleur-journal/giro-ditalia-2022-stage-four-preview)
- Bases: Nicolosi (south side), Linguaglossa (north side), Catania (airport) — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-sicily-the-etna-region-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/)

**Warnings**
- Surfaces are rough in places, with potholes and lava grit at the road edge, so wider tyres are advised — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-sicily-the-etna-region-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/)
- It is "about 10 degrees lower when you get to the top of Etna than in Taormina" — [Cycling UK forum: Mount Etna – how high can you go?](https://forum.cyclinguk.org/viewtopic.php?t=71999), old
- Etna is an active volcano. BikeRadar's writer rode down it "weeks before it erupted" — [BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/mount-etna-eruption)

## Crete

**Verdict**
- Cycling Weekly lists Crete among "eight alternative European islands" — [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/eight-perfect-european-islands-for-cycling-alternative-485781), ~2020–21
- Spring is "one of the best times to explore by bike". Summer middays reach 35 °C or more — [12 practical tips for cycling holidays in Greece](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-holidays-in-greece/), Epic Road Rides

**Routes and climbs**
- **Skinakas:** 45 km from the coast to the observatory at 1,752 m, with sections above 16% but an average of only ~4%. The Lakkoi climb has seven switchbacks in just 900 m — [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/eight-perfect-european-islands-for-cycling-alternative-485781)

**Warnings**
- Gradients are "often in double digits", so a compact chainset or wide-range cassette is recommended — [Epic Road Rides Greece](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/cycling-holidays-in-greece/)
- **Conflict on roads:** one rider says roads are "not great" once you leave Heraklion or Chania. Another says they are "mostly well surfaced and the gradients kind". Map roads may no longer exist or may be fenced off — [road.cc forum: Cycling in Crete – what bike?](https://road.cc/content/forum/cycling-crete-what-bike-take-306571), [Cycling UK forum](https://forum.cyclinguk.org/viewtopic.php?t=96966), both old

## Antalya (Turkey)

- The Lycian Coast is an April pick: "spring brings lush, green hillsides and a burst of wildflowers" — [Spring cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/spring-cycling-holidays/) / [Best cycling holidays in April](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/best-places-for-cycling-holidays-in-april/), Epic Road Rides
- In Kemer, the Kemer → Beldibi coastal route is the most-cited road ride. Mount Tahtalı (Olympos) routes are "a unique challenge". Saklıkent ski resort is under an hour from the coast — [Go Türkiye: cycling](https://goturkiye.com/cycling/mountain) (**tourism board, not cycling media**)
- Antalya hosts a UCI Gran Fondo World Series event. Named climbs are Geyikbayırı/Çakırlar (Taurus foothills) and Termessos — [Il Sole 24 Ore: cycling without seasons – Antalya](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/turkey-in-antalya-bike-triathlon-ski-slopes-and-swimming-AImfxnw), [Uğur Bisiklet blog](https://ugurbisiklet.com/en/blogs/bisiklet-blog/antalya-da-bisiklet-surulecek-en-iyi-5-rota-sahil-yolundan-toroslara)

## Marrakech / High Atlas

- **Oukaïmeden** is the main climb near Marrakech: "a long, sustained effort where pacing matters", with wind increasing as shelter disappears higher up — [ProCyclingUK: Cycling in the High Atlas from Marrakech](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-the-high-atlas-from-marrakech-the-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/)
- **Tizi n'Test** (~2,092 m): "about the best road to bike in all of Morocco". Watch for sheer drops at the road edge and gravel or sand on the surface. Some accounts say the top section is being repaired. Riders often stay overnight at guesthouses near the summit. A sportive queen stage runs Tahnaout → Tizi n'Test (103 km, 1,390 m) — [Sportive Breaks: Tizi n'Test](https://sportivebreaks.com/cycling-climbs/tizi-n-test/), [Sportive Breaks: High Atlas loop](https://sportivebreaks.com/blog/cycling-in-morocco-the-high-atlas-loop/)
- Season runs from **mid-February to May**, before it gets too hot. Start early, because traffic picks up later in the day — [Sportive Breaks](https://sportivebreaks.com/blog/cycling-in-morocco-the-high-atlas-loop/), [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-the-high-atlas-from-marrakech-the-climbs-roads-and-atmosphere-that-make-it-special/)
- Morocco is on Epic Road Rides' list of Christmas and New Year cycling holidays — [7 must-do Christmas cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/christmas-new-year-cycling-holidays/)

## Tuscany

- Best months: Cycling Weekly says **March–May** (highs of 15–23 °C) — [Cycling Weekly](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/cycling-holiday-365786), ~2023
- Epic Road Rides prefers **April–May**. One source warns that April often turns out wet, while May and June are more reliable — [Spring cycling holidays](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/spring-cycling-holidays/), [ProCyclingUK: Riding through Tuscany](https://procyclinguk.com/riding-through-tuscany-chiantis-rolling-hills-strade-bianche-and-vineyard-routes/)
- Bases: **Gaiole in Chianti** ("a natural base for serious cyclists"), with Radda, Greve or Panzano as alternatives. Siena is the hub for the Strade Bianche. A sample loop of ~65 km runs Gaiole → Radda → Volpaia → Panzano → Greve with a gravel section — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/riding-through-tuscany-chiantis-rolling-hills-strade-bianche-and-vineyard-routes/)
- Use tyres of at least 28 mm for the firmer white roads. Loose vineyard tracks need a gravel bike — [ProCyclingUK](https://procyclinguk.com/riding-through-tuscany-chiantis-rolling-hills-strade-bianche-and-vineyard-routes/)
- Other guides: [Cycling Tuscany guide](https://epicroadrides.com/destinations/cycling-italy/tuscany/) (Epic Road Rides) and the [Saddle Skedaddle Strade Bianche Explorer](https://www.cyclist.co.uk/sponsored/saddle-skedaddle-strade-bianche-explorer) (Cyclist, **sponsored**)

## Georgia (Kakheti)

## UAE (Ras Al Khaimah / Jebel Jais)

- "For riders searching for somewhere genuinely different during the European winter, Dubai and Jebel Jais deserve more attention" — [ProCyclingUK: Dubai desert loops & Jebel Jais](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-dubai-desert-loops-jebel-jais-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/)
- Climb figures differ by source:
  - Café du Cycliste: ~35 km at a "smooth" 4.1%
  - PJAMM: 16.7 miles at 5.1%, from 315 m to 1,346 m at the viewing deck

  - It is a tourist road and much busier at weekends
  - Above a roadblock near the top, only cyclists, pedestrians and permit holders may continue
  - Goats cross the road in groups on the descent
  - The Café du Cycliste rider went in March and started early to avoid the heat and Dubai traffic

## Oman
