# Flight connections PRG / VIE (/ BTS) to winter-spring road-cycling destinations, Jan–May 2027

Research date: 22 Oct 2026. Method note: web search worked, but direct page fetches failed with DNS errors for almost every site (aviation24.be, e15.cz, cestujlevne.com, zaletsi.cz, flightconnections.com, news.at, nomadlawyer.org). So the findings below rest on search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. Treat every frequency and fare as **indicative** and check it in the booking engine before buying. I could not establish airport-by-airport coverage for all 28 destinations. The Gaps sections list what is still unverified.

## 1. Which routes are confirmed for winter 2026/27 and summer 2027 from PRG, VIE and BTS?

### Takeaway
Winter 2026/27 is a **contracting** season for low-cost carriers in this region. Wizz Air has closed its Vienna base. Ryanair has cut 10 Vienna routes, including Lanzarote, and suspended its winter operations at Chania and Heraklion. Smartwings is trimming Prague–Málaga and Prague–Palma. The firmest options are:
- **Prague:** Smartwings to the Canaries, Madeira, Málaga and the new Lisbon route.
- **Vienna:** Austrian Airlines to Málaga, Tenerife, Larnaca and Funchal.
- **Bratislava:** Ryanair, whose 23-route winter network includes Paphos, Málaga, Lanzarote, Malta, Palermo, Alicante and Barcelona.

## 3. Typical fares and baggage policy

### Takeaway
I found only "from" fares. Smartwings Lisbon is from €116 one-way with a full-size cabin bag, so about 5,700+ CZK return. Smartwings Funchal is from 2,960 CZK one-way. Prague–Marrakech and Prague–Agadir are from about 1,050–1,270 CZK one-way on metasearch. A realistic 6–10-week-ahead return fare with a cabin bag is not documented.

### Cited Findings
- Smartwings PRG–LIS from €116 one-way, which **includes a full-size cabin bag** — [aviation24.be](https://www.aviation24.be/airlines/smartwings/smartwings-adds-prague-lisbon-route-and-expands-european-network-for-winter-2026-27/)
- Smartwings PRG–FNC "from 2,960 CZK" — [smartwings.com](https://www.smartwings.com/letenky-praha-funchal)
- PRG–RAK from 1,045 CZK one-way — [momondo.cz](https://www.momondo.cz/lety/praha/marrakes). easyJet sale fare 2,023 CZK — [jakdoafriky.cz](https://jakdoafriky.cz/akcni-letenky-praha-marrakes-2023kc/)
- PRG–AGA from 1,264 CZK — [momondo.cz](https://www.momondo.cz/lety/praha/agadir)

### Inferences
- "From" fares are teaser minima. For 5–6 people on fixed dates booked 6–10 weeks ahead, these return-with-cabin-bag fares are an **estimate, not sourced**:
  - **Short and medium haul (Italy, Spain, Malta, Cyprus via BTS):** roughly 3,000–7,000 CZK.
  - **Canaries, Madeira and Lisbon on Smartwings:** roughly 6,000–12,000 CZK.
  - Peak periods cost more: school holidays (jarní prázdniny, Feb–Mar) and Easter (Easter Sunday is 28 Mar 2027).
- Since the group rents bikes, no sports-equipment fees apply. On Ryanair the cheapest fare includes only an under-seat bag; a 10 kg cabin trolley needs Priority or Regular. This is general knowledge and was not sourced in this session.

### Gaps
- No sourced bag-fee schedules for Ryanair, Smartwings, Austrian or Eurowings.
- No Google Flights or Kiwi price histories could be retrieved.
