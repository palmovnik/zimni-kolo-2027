# Food and drink at winter/spring cycling destinations (for a group of food-loving cyclists)

Scope: 16 locations, trip January–April 2027 (May possible), group of 5–6 Czech road cyclists. Research done October 2026 with about 20 web searches. Searches mostly returned summaries, and few primary pages were fetched in full, so treat names as leads to check on guide.michelin.com before booking. Anything marked "(inference / background knowledge)" is not backed by a source found in this session.

## Q1: Which locations combine the best cycling with the best food?

### Takeaway
The best combinations of cycling and food are Girona/Costa Brava, Costa Blanca (Calpe/Dénia), Mallorca, Sicily/Etna and Andalusia (Málaga/Ronda/Marbella). Each has several Michelin-starred restaurants, a strong everyday tapas or trattoria culture and wine regions within riding distance. Tenerife has improved a lot in fine dining and is the strongest of the Canary Islands. Madeira and the Algarve are solid mid-tier choices. The Antalya region and Marrakech/High Atlas rank lowest for this group: Antalya because of all-inclusive hotel food and no Michelin coverage found, Marrakech because alcohol is limited.

### Cited Findings

**Mallorca — rating 4/5, price €€–€€€**

This comes from a secondary blog, and its green-star count conflicts with another source (4 vs. 4+1) — [Imperial Properties](https://www.imperial-properties.com/en/ImperialPropertiesBlog/restaurantes-estrella-michelin-en-mallorca-2026-la-guia-completa); [Eat Drink Sleep Mallorca](https://eatdrinksleepmallorca.com/2025/11/26/mallorcas-michelin-stars-for-2026/)

Bens d'Avall (Sóller) has 1 star and a green star — [ViaMichelin](https://www.viamichelin.com/maps/restaurants/poi/soller-07100-fa3ea05a); [Imperial Properties](https://www.imperial-properties.com/en/ImperialPropertiesBlog/restaurantes-estrella-michelin-en-mallorca-2026-la-guia-completa)
- Port de Pollença: Terrae (chef David Rivas) received a new green star for 2026 — [Eat Drink Sleep Mallorca](https://eatdrinksleepmallorca.com/2025/11/26/mallorcas-michelin-stars-for-2026/)

Cycling Planet (Alaró) is open "pretty much all year round". Hotel Llenaire welcomes "large groups of hungry cyclists" — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/mallorcas-best-cafes-coffee-stops/); [Palma Weekly](https://www.palmaweekly.com/?p=10394)

**Girona / Costa Brava — rating 5/5, price €€–€€€**
- La Fábrica (Carrer de la Llebre 3), run by former pro Christian Meier and Amber, is a cycling café with specialty coffee opened in 2014 and a fixture among road cyclists. Federal (Carrer de la Força 9) is good for brunch. Other picks: Eat Sleep Cycle café, Idle Hands, La Comuna, Coffee & Greens, Hors Categorie, Bionèctar — [Eat Sleep Cycle](https://www.eatsleepcycle.com/cyclists-guide-girona); [Family Adventure Project](https://www.familyadventureproject.org/la-fabrica-girona-cycle-cafe/amp/)
- (inference / background knowledge) Girona has El Celler de Can Roca (3 Michelin stars, Roca brothers), and the Rocas also run more affordable places in town. The Empordà DO wine region and the Costa Brava fish towns are within riding distance. Check current star status on guide.michelin.com.

**Costa Blanca (Calpe/Dénia/Altea) — rating 4.5/5, price €€–€€€**
- Starred restaurants in 2026: Quique Dacosta (Dénia, 3 stars). Audrey's, Beat and Orobianco in Calpe (1 star each; Orobianco is Italian with a view of the Peñón de Ifach). Peix & Brases (Dénia, 1 star, grill and market cooking). Source is a villa rental agency's blog whose table "appears to have some errors" — [Premium Villas Costa Blanca](https://www.premium-villas-costa-blanca.com/blog/michelin-star-restaurants-alicante-2026/); [La Marina Alta](https://en.lamarinaalta.com/estrellas-michelin-guia-marina-alta/)
- Spain 2026 has 204 Bib Gourmands nationally, 29 of them new. I did not find a list for Calpe or Dénia — [Michelin Guide ES](https://guide.michelin.com/es/es/articulo/michelin-guide-ceremony/nuevos-bib-gourmand-de-la-guia-michelin-espana-2026); [Michelin press PDF](https://espacioprensa.michelin.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/CP_Guia-MICHELIN-Espana-2026_ES.pdf)
- (inference / background knowledge) Key dishes are arroz (arroz a banda, arroz del senyoret), Dénia red prawn (gamba roja de Dénia) and Alicante/Valencian wines (Monastrell, Moscatel from the Marina Alta).

**Andalusia (Málaga/Ronda/Marbella) — rating 4.5/5, price €–€€€**
- Bardal (Ronda, chef Benito Gómez) and Skina (Marbella) each hold 2 Michelin stars, and both chefs coordinated the Michelin Spain 2026 gala in Málaga (25 Nov 2025). Málaga province has about 10 starred restaurants (Marbella, Fuengirola, Ronda, Málaga); sources differ on 9 vs. 10 — [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/gastropoli/gala-michelin-2026-en-malaga-fechas-hora-restaurantes-y-estrellas-que-marcaran-la-alta-cocina-espanola.html); [Marbesol](https://www.marbesol.com/en/blog/gastronomy/michelin-starred-restaurants-in-malaga); [Michelin press PDF Málaga](https://espacioprensa.michelin.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Ndp_Malaga-host-city.pdf)
- Málaga wines served at the gala dinner included Mountain Blanco (Málaga) and Molino Real (Axarquía) — [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/gastropoli/gala-michelin-2026-en-malaga-fechas-hora-restaurantes-y-estrellas-que-marcaran-la-alta-cocina-espanola.html)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food: tapas, pescaíto frito, espetos de sardinas, ajoblanco. Wine regions are DO Sierras de Málaga (Serranía de Ronda) and sweet Málaga wines. My searches did not confirm which Ronda wineries take visitors (see Gaps).

**Sicily (Catania / Taormina / Etna) — rating 5/5 for food, 4.5 overall, price €–€€**
- Tenute dei Ciclopi runs an Etna wine tasting room in central Catania, open Wed–Sun 11:00–01:00 and closed Mon–Tue — [WineTourism](https://www.winetourism.com/winery/tenute-dei-ciclopi/); [Winedering](https://www.winedering.com/the-myth-etna-wine-tasting-in-the-heart-of-catania_d9977_travel)
- (inference / background knowledge) Etna DOC (Nerello Mascalese, Carricante) is one of Europe's most fashionable wine regions. The wineries sit on the north side (Passopisciaro, Solicchiata, Randazzo, Linguaglossa), right on climbing routes around Etna. Starred restaurants have included Shalai (Linguaglossa) and Zash (Riposto). Typical food: arancini, pasta alla Norma, granita with brioche, cannoli, pistachio from Bronte, plus the Catania fish market. I did not verify any Michelin status in this session.

**Tenerife — rating 4/5, price €–€€€**
- New first Michelin stars went to Haydée (La Orotava), Taste 1973 (Playa de las Américas) and El Taller de Seve Díaz (Puerto de la Cruz). M.B. by Martín Berasategui (Ritz-Carlton Abama) has 2 stars. Sources give different totals (7 vs. 10–11 stars) — [Selling Travel](https://www.sellingtravel.co.uk/tenerifes-new-michelin-stars-to-attract-foodies/); [Teneriffa News](https://www.teneriffa-news.com/en/news/new-michelin-stars-tenerife_1463432.html); [Webtenerife](https://www.webtenerife.co.uk/blog/2025/01/the-paradise-of-michelin-stars/)
- Guachinches are rustic eateries attached to wineries, mostly in the north of the island, and El Primero is one of the best known. El Taller also makes its own wines with Bodega Tajinaste — [Meliá blog](https://www.melia.com/es/blog/viajar/mejores-guachinches-de-tenerife) (page has a mismatched title, so use with caution); [Teneriffa News](https://www.teneriffa-news.com/en/news/el-taller-tajinaste-tenerife-wines_1464420.html)

**Lanzarote — rating 3.5/5, price €–€€**
- El Grifo (founded 1775) is the oldest bodega in the Canaries. Its museum is open Mon–Fri 10–18, and tours are by appointment. Kamezí holds a Michelin star — [La Voz de Lanzarote](https://www.lavozdelanzarote.com/en/ekonomus/gourmet/grifo-brings-together-in-lanzarote-the-owners-of-eight-michelin-starred-restaurants_235398_102.html)

**La Palma — rating 3/5, price €**
- Restaurants cluster in the Aridane Valley, including Pizca Bistrot and Burg On in Los Llanos. Older sources also list Casona de Argual, Balcón Taburiente and Bodegón Tamanca (in a wine cave), so check these are still open — [IslaGuru (July 2026)](https://islaguru.com/articles/restaurants-la-palma); [ING/IAC list](https://www.ing.iac.es/PR/lapalma/restaurants.html)

Roads around Las Manchas may still be affected by the 2021 lava flows — [Hello Canary Islands](https://www.hellocanaryislands.com/events/la-palma/festival-saborea-la-palma-me-sabe); [wine-pages forum](https://wine-pages.com/community/threads/la-palma-la-gomera-canary-islands-tips.13129/latest)

**Cyprus (Paphos / Limassol / Troodos) — rating 3.5/5, price €–€€**
- I found no official Michelin guide for Cyprus. Almyra (Paphos) is a "Michelin Selected" hotel. In Limassol, Ristorante Locatelli (AMARA) and The Polo (Alasia) are run with starred chefs — [Star Wine List](https://starwinelist.com/wine-guide/great-wine-places-in-cyprus); [En Primeur Club Limassol](https://www.enprimeurclub.com/cities/limassol)
- Troodos wine villages: Omodos and Koilani have about 10 wineries between them. Commandaria is one of the oldest named wines in the world, a sweet wine from sun-dried grapes, and its wine route covers 14 villages. Vouni Panayia winery (Pano Panayia) offers a tour and tasting of indigenous grape varieties and a restaurant that needs a reservation, and Fikardos is on the way — [Sunvil](https://www.sunvil.co.uk/blog/wine-villages-of-cyprus); [Decanter](https://www.decanter.com/wine-travel/cyprus-travel-guide-restaurants-wineries-and-hotels-478805/); [Cyprus Mail](https://cyprus-mail.com/2021/10/01/the-best-place-in-cyprus-for-the-wine-lovers-troodos-wine-routes)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food: meze (in a taverna it often means 15–20 dishes for a fixed price), halloumi, souvla, kleftiko.

**Madeira — rating 4/5, price €–€€€**
- Il Gallo d'Oro (Funchal) has 2 stars and a green star. William and Desarma (Funchal) have 1 star each, confirmed only by a pre-ceremony source. The Portugal 2026 gala was held in Funchal on 10 March 2026 — [DN](https://www.dn.pt/viver/madeira-acolhe-próxima-edição-da-gala-michelin-em-portugal-cerimónia-distingue-melhores-restaurantes-do-país); [Lisboa Secreta](https://lisboasecreta.co/en/michelin-guide-portugal-2026-list/); [Michelin](https://www.michelin.com/en/publications/products-and-services/one-new-two-star-restaurant-shines-in-the-michelin-guide-portugal-2026)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food and drink: espetada, black scabbardfish (espada) with banana, bolo do caco, poncha, Madeira wine (Blandy's Wine Lodge in Funchal).

**Crete (Chania) — rating 4/5, price €**
- I found no Michelin listing for Chania. Tamam is set in an Ottoman bathhouse in Splantzia, is open year-round and serves lamb with wild greens for about €16. Topanas is a sommelier's Cretan restaurant with a large wine cellar. To Maridaki serves small fish for under €12. Portes (behind the market hall) has mains at €11–17. Thalassino Ageri is a fish restaurant in Koum Kapi, closed Mondays — [crete.direct](https://crete.direct/articles/best-tavernas-chania); [Greek Trip Planner](https://greektriplanner.me/blog/best-restaurants-in-chania); [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/europe/greece/crete/places/chania/restaurants)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food and drink: dakos, kalitsounia, raki/tsikoudia served free at the end of a meal, staka, olive oil. The cuisine relies heavily on local produce.

**Antalya region — rating 2.5/5 if you eat in hotels, 3.5/5 outside them, price €–€€**
- The Michelin Guide Türkiye 2026 covers Istanbul, Izmir, Muğla and Cappadocia. Antalya is not in the selection. A first nationwide selection has been announced for the following edition — [Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/gb/en/article/michelin-guide-ceremony/the-michelin-guide-turkiye-reveals-its-2026-restaurant-selection)
- (inference / background knowledge) Cycling hotels around Antalya/Belek are mostly all-inclusive, so food is buffet-style and abundant but generic. Kaleiçi (the old town) has better restaurants. Signature dishes: piyaz (Antalya style), meze, grilled fish, Turkish breakfast.

**Marrakech / High Atlas — rating 3/5, price €–€€**
- Most restaurants in Marrakech do not serve alcohol. Al Fassia (Guéliz, run by women, Fassi cuisine) has a wide choice of Moroccan wines. Plus 61 (Guéliz) also has Moroccan wines. El Fenn's rooftop serves cocktails, and Café Arabe serves alcohol. Nomad is rated "best of 2026" by an aggregator, but I could not confirm it serves alcohol — [Fodor's](https://www.fodors.com/world/africa-and-middle-east/morocco/marrakesh/hotels/fodors-choice); [World Travel Guide](https://worldtravelguide.net/guides/africa/morocco/marrakech/restaurants); [TripAdvisor forum](https://www.tripadvisor.co.nz/ShowTopic-g293734-i9196-k8190904-Restaurant_that_serve_alcohol_recommendations-Marrakech_Marrakech_Safi.html)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food: tagine, couscous (traditionally on Fridays), pastilla, tanjia, mint tea. In the High Atlas you eat in kasbah guesthouses, usually without alcohol.

**Tuscany (Siena / Val d'Orcia) — rating 5/5 in season, 3.5/5 in January–February, price €€–€€€**
- Osteria Perillà (Rocca d'Orcia) is listed as a "tiny Michelin-starred restaurant", but the source is from around 2018, so the star is uncertain. Gambero Rosso recommends Campo del Drago (Rosewood Castiglion del Bosco, Montalcino) and Osteria del Leone (Bagno Vignoni) — [Goop](https://goop.com/place/italy/rocca-dorcia/osteria-perilla); [Gambero Rosso](https://www.gamberorossointernational.com/?p=507221)
- Winter is the off-season, and many restaurants and shops in Montepulciano are closed (undated blog) — [Upstate International](https://upstateinternational.org/?p=60946)
- (inference / background knowledge) Signature food and drink: pici, bistecca, pecorino di Pienza, Brunello di Montalcino, Vino Nobile di Montepulciano, Chianti Classico.

### Inferences
- Ranking for "best cycling + best food" (my synthesis): 1) Girona, 2) Sicily/Etna (best value and wine, but colder weather and less reliable in Jan–Feb), 3) Costa Blanca (Calpe has three 1-star restaurants within walking distance of the cycling hotels and Dénia has Quique Dacosta), 4) Mallorca, 5) Andalusia. Tuscany has top food but is mainly a choice for April–May.
- Price: the Iberian and Greek options are cheapest for everyday eating (€). Tenerife, Mallorca and the Algarve can easily reach €€€ at fine-dining level.

### Gaps

- Ronda wineries that take visitors were not found.
- I found no reliable restaurant recommendations for Antalya outside the hotels, and nothing on cycling cafés in Cyprus, Crete or Madeira.

## Q2: What is open in the off-season (January–February)?

### Takeaway
Cities and places with a year-round population are safest in Jan–Feb: Girona, Catania, Málaga, Funchal, Las Palmas, Chania and Limassol. Resort areas (Port de Pollença, Calpe in part, Belek, Val d'Orcia, parts of the Algarve) partly close, and cycling cafés in Mallorca may have reduced hours.

### Cited Findings
- Mallorca: the cycling season starts in February and is busiest until May, and most shops stay open until October. Shop cafés may therefore be closed in Jan. Tolo's was found closed by one reviewer. Cycling Planet (Alaró) is open almost all year — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/mallorcas-best-cafes-coffee-stops/); [Casa Pilot](https://www.casapilot.com/en/blog/mallorca-cycling-holiday-guide)
- Chania: Tamam is open year-round — [crete.direct](https://crete.direct/articles/best-tavernas-chania)
- Tuscany: many restaurants in Montepulciano are closed in winter — [Upstate International](https://upstateinternational.org/?p=60946)
- Catania: the Tenute dei Ciclopi tasting room is open Wed–Sun (no seasonal closure mentioned) — [WineTourism](https://www.winetourism.com/winery/tenute-dei-ciclopi/)
- Lanzarote: the El Grifo museum is open Mon–Fri 10–18 (no seasonal closure mentioned) — [La Voz de Lanzarote](https://www.lavozdelanzarote.com/en/ekonomus/gourmet/grifo-brings-together-in-lanzarote-the-owners-of-eight-michelin-starred-restaurants_235398_102.html)

### Inferences
- (inference / background knowledge) Many Spanish and Italian fine-dining restaurants close for 2–6 weeks in January for staff holidays (in recent years Quique Dacosta has only been open seasonally, roughly spring to autumn). Check before booking. Fine-dining restaurants on Mallorca often reopen only in February–March.
- (inference / background knowledge) Spain: lunch is 13:30–15:30 and dinner from 20:30–21:00. A Czech group used to eating at 18:00 will find restaurants closed and should use tapas bars or "merienda". Spain's practical advantage is a big lunch after riding (menú del día for €15–20).
- (inference / background knowledge) Antalya/Belek: in Jan–Feb some hotels close or open only for sports groups, and the town of Belek is quiet. Kaleiçi in Antalya is open year-round.
- (inference / background knowledge) Canaries and Madeira: there is no real off-season (winter is the peak season), so everything is open.
- (inference / background knowledge) Morocco: alcohol is not served during Ramadan or is hard to get. Ramadan 2027 is expected around 8 Feb – 9 Mar 2027 (approximate dates, check), which affects the Marrakech/High Atlas option in February.

### Gaps
- I did not find concrete 2027 opening dates or holiday closures for individual restaurants. These are usually published only in December.

## Q3: Which wineries and food experiences can be combined with a ride?

### Takeaway
The strongest "ride to a winery" options are Etna (wineries right on the volcano's climbs), Troodos/Commandaria (wine villages on climbs from Limassol/Paphos), Lanzarote's La Geria (a flat lava landscape with bodegas next to each other), Val d'Orcia (Brunello/Nobile, April–May), Tenerife's guachinches in the north and Mallorca (Binissalem DO in the middle of the island).

### Cited Findings
- Lanzarote, La Geria: El Grifo, Rubicón (with a bar), Bodega La Geria, Stratvs, Los Bermejos and El Chupadero (tasting with food) all sit on one road — [Lanzarote Guidebook](https://www.lanzaroteguidebook.com/information/food-and-drink/lanzarote-bodegas); [Lanzarote3](https://lanzarote3.com/en/the-wine-route-through-la-geria/?amp=1)
- Cyprus: Fikardos, then Vouni Panayia (Pano Panayia), with a tour, tasting and restaurant (book ahead). Omodos and Koilani have about 10 wineries. The Commandaria route covers 14 villages near Kolossi Castle — [Decanter](https://www.decanter.com/wine-travel/cyprus-travel-guide-restaurants-wineries-and-hotels-478805/); [Sunvil](https://www.sunvil.co.uk/blog/wine-villages-of-cyprus)
- Tenerife: guachinches at wineries in the north (El Primero). El Taller de Seve Díaz with Bodega Tajinaste — [Meliá blog](https://www.melia.com/es/blog/viajar/mejores-guachinches-de-tenerife); [Teneriffa News](https://www.teneriffa-news.com/en/news/el-taller-tajinaste-tenerife-wines_1464420.html)
- La Palma: Bodegas Teneguía (Fuencaliente) for Malvasía, which pairs well with a ride to the southern tip of the island — [wine-pages forum](https://wine-pages.com/community/threads/la-palma-la-gomera-canary-islands-tips.13129/latest)
- Catania: an Etna tasting in the city after riding (Tenute dei Ciclopi) — [WineTourism](https://www.winetourism.com/winery/tenute-dei-ciclopi/)
- Mallorca: Ca'n Topa on Coll de Sóller as a lunch stop, and the menu at Ca'n Boqueta in Sóller after descending the Tramuntana — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/mallorcas-best-cafes-coffee-stops/); [ViaMichelin](https://www.viamichelin.com/maps/restaurants/poi/soller-07100-fa3ea05a)

### Inferences
- (inference / background knowledge) Etna: a loop from Catania or Linguaglossa over the north side of the volcano passes Passopisciaro and Solicchiata (Benanti, Planeta Feudo di Mezzo, Passopisciaro, Graci, etc.). You need to book tastings in advance, and in Jan–Feb some have limited hours.
- (inference / background knowledge) Girona: a ride into the Empordà (Peralada, Garriguella) for a winery visit, and lunch by the sea in Calella de Palafrugell or l'Escala (anchovies).
- (inference / background knowledge) Costa Blanca: Bodegas in the Jalón valley (Xaló) are on classic cycling routes from Calpe/Dénia, and the Dénia fish market is worth a visit.
- (inference / background knowledge) Andalusia: Ronda wineries (DO Sierras de Málaga) after the climbs from Marbella or San Pedro; Málaga's Mercado de Atarazanas.
- Practical points: book tastings ahead for 6 people. In Cyprus and Lanzarote, tasting during the ride is fine; in Tuscany and Etna it is better as the last stop before the hotel or with a support car.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed which wineries take walk-in cyclists in Jan–Feb 2027.
- Ronda: no specific wineries confirmed.
