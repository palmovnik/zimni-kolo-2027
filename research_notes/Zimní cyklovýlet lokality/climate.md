# Climate Jan–May for winter/spring road-cycling destinations (Southern Europe, Canaries, Madeira, Turkey, Morocco)

> **IMPORTANT – read first (method and reliability).** In this research session, outbound access to Wikipedia, AEMET, IPMA, climate-data.org, weatherspark and every other climate site was **blocked by the network proxy** (HTTP 403 / DNS failure), so no primary table could be opened directly. The only working tool was a web-search engine that returns summarised snippets. As a result:
> - Values marked **[V]** were confirmed in this session from search results, and the source is linked.
> - Values marked **[~]** are **indicative values from the researcher's background knowledge** of the official station normals (mostly AEMET 1981–2010 / 1991–2020, IPMA, HNMS, MGM, Meteo Malta, as reproduced in Wikipedia climate boxes). They were **NOT re-verified in this session**. They are good enough to rank destinations (typically ±1 °C, ±20–30 % for rain and sunshine), but should be checked against the named station before being published as exact figures.
> - **Day length** was calculated astronomically (sunrise to sunset, mid-month, by latitude), so it does not need a source.
> - **Sea temperatures** are typical monthly means, rounded to 0.5 °C [~]. No authoritative SST table could be reached. NOAA OISST and Copernicus Marine are the recommended check.

## Q1 – Monthly climate tables per location (Jan / Feb / Mar / Apr / May)

### Takeaway
The Canaries (Tenerife S, Gran Canaria S, Lanzarote, Fuerteventura) are the only places that are reliably about 21–24 °C and dry (≤3 rain days/month) from January. Madeira is about 20 °C but wetter. Marrakech and Agadir are warm and sunny but have cold mornings (Marrakech) or occasional Atlantic rain. Cyprus, Málaga, Alicante and Algarve reach about 17–18 °C in Jan–Feb and become comfortable (≥20 °C) in late March–April. Mallorca, Sicily, Crete, Malta and Sardinia are about 15–16 °C in Jan–Feb and comfortable from April. Girona and Siena are cool (≤14 °C, near-freezing mornings) until April–May.

## Q2 – Which places are reliably ≥18 °C and dry in Jan–Feb, and which only from March/April?

### Takeaway
Only the Canary Islands (especially the south of Tenerife and Gran Canaria, Lanzarote and Fuerteventura) and, with more rain and cloud, Madeira are reliably ≥18 °C and dry in Jan–Feb. Marrakech and Agadir have ≥19 °C afternoons and are sunny but have cold mornings or rare rain. Cyprus, Málaga, Costa Blanca and the Algarve reach 17–18 °C in Jan–Feb, with 3–9 rain days. They reach a dependable ≥20 °C only from late March/April. Mallorca, Sicily, Crete, Malta, Sardinia and Antalya are April destinations. Girona and Siena are late April–May destinations.

### Cited Findings
- Tenerife Sur: Jan–Mar max 21.7–23.1 °C, with only about 2 rain days a month — [AEMET C429I](https://www.aemet.es/en/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=C429I&amp=&k=coo)
- Canary Islands winter 2025–26 was "normal" (+0.2 °C), while Baleares were "very warm" (+1.6 °C) — [MITECO/AEMET winter 2025–26](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/marzo/el-invierno-tuvo-caracter-muy-calido-y-muy-humedo.html); [AEMET avance invierno 2025–26 (PDF)](https://www.aemet.es/documentos/es/noticias/2026/03/avance_climatico_nacional_invierno_2025_2026.pdf)
- Funchal Jan–Mar has 5.5–6.2 rain days per month (≥1 mm) — [IPMA Funchal 1991–2020](https://www.ipma.pt/bin/file.data/climate-normal/cn_91-20_FUNCHAL.pdf)
- Marrakech Jan max 19.1 °C, but min 5.9 °C — [climatestotravel Marrakesh](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/morocco/marrakesh)
- Paphos Feb max 17.6 °C with about 60 mm rain — [villaprasino](https://www.villaprasino.com/guides/paphos-weather/)
- Antalya January: 217 mm over about 13 wet days (1991–2020) — [climatememory](https://climatememory.com/en/climate/republic-of-turkiye/antalya)
- Catania (Sigonella) max 15.9–16.2 °C in Jan–Feb, rising to 21.4 °C in April — [climatestotravel Catania](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/italy/catania)

### Inferences
Comfort classes for a 4–5 night trip (mean max / rain days):
- **Jan–Feb ≥20 °C, ≤3 rain days:** Tenerife S (Adeje), Gran Canaria S (Maspalomas), Lanzarote, Fuerteventura. Agadir is ~21 °C and sunny, but its data are weaker.
- **Jan–Feb ~19–21 °C but wetter or with cold mornings:** Madeira (Funchal, ~6 rain days, cloudy mountains), La Palma (east side wetter; Los Llanos better), Marrakech (19–21 °C but 6–8 °C starts).
- **Jan–Feb ~16.5–18 °C, OK from mid-March:** Málaga/Marbella, Costa Blanca (Calpe/Alicante, the driest of the Spanish Mediterranean), Algarve, Paphos/Limassol.
- **≥19–20 °C only from April:** Mallorca, Sicily (Catania), Crete, Malta, Sardinia, Antalya (wettest in Jan–Feb).
- **Late April–May only:** Girona, Tuscany (Siena), Ronda inland.
- Wind matters as much as temperature on the eastern Canaries (Lanzarote, Fuerteventura).

### Gaps
- None of the sources gives a "% of days ≥18 °C" statistic. The classification is based on monthly means.

## Q3 – Mountain temperature drop and snow / road-closure risk on key climbs

### Takeaway
Plan on a drop of about 0.6–0.7 °C per 100 m: 6–7 °C colder at 1,000 m and 12–14 °C colder at 2,000 m than at the coast. Wind chill on fast descents comes on top of that. In Jan–Mar, snow or ice closures occur most winters on Teide (TF-21/TF-24), Troodos/Olympus, Etna (Rifugio Sapienza), Sierra Nevada (above Hoya de la Mora closed to traffic), Oukaïmeden and Saklıkent. Pico do Arieiro and Roque de los Muchachos close occasionally, a few days per winter. Tizi n'Tichka closes for about 1–3 days per year.

## Q4 – Variability: how often cold/rainy spells occur (Girona, Sicily, Crete, Jan–Mar)

### Takeaway
North-Mediterranean and island destinations (Girona, Siena, Sicily, Crete, Malta, Sardinia, Antalya) get winter weather in spells. One or two Atlantic or Mediterranean lows per month can bring 2–4 days of rain, wind and highs of 10–13 °C. A 4–5 night trip in Jan–Feb therefore has a substantial chance of losing 1–2 days. The Canaries have much less variability, although in winter Atlantic lows ("DANA"/"borrasca") and Saharan dust (calima) cause occasional rainy or windy days.

### Cited Findings
- Spain winter 2025–26 was "very wet" overall (and warm), February was +2.4 °C, and January was normal — [MITECO/AEMET](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/marzo/el-invierno-tuvo-caracter-muy-calido-y-muy-humedo.html); [maldita.es](https://maldita.es/clima/20260203/que-dijo-y-que-no-dijo-la-aemet-sobre-el-invierno-2025-2026/)
- Girona monthly rainfall varies hugely between years: one 2025 dataset shows January at 16 mm versus a normal of about 54–60 mm — [statsclimat](https://statsclimat.com/Europe/Spain/report_Girona); [climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/spain/girona)
- Antalya January rain (about 217 mm) is concentrated in about 10–13 wet days — [climatememory](https://climatememory.com/en/climate/republic-of-turkiye/antalya)
- Malta: 10 rain days (≥1 mm) in January vs 4 in April — [Luqa climate box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luqa)
- Etna and Troodos see snowstorms in Jan–Mar (2025, 2023), and even in May (2026, Troodos) — see the Q3 sources

### Inferences
- Rain days per 5-day stay in Jan–Feb (rain days per month × 5/30): Canaries S ≈0.3–0.5; Alicante ≈0.5; Mallorca, Málaga, Faro, Funchal ≈0.8–1.0; Girona, Cagliari, Sicily ≈1; Paphos ≈1.2–1.5; Heraklion ≈1.5; Malta, Chania ≈1.7–2; Antalya ≈2.
- Girona: tramontana episodes and cold valley mornings (frost to about 0 °C) are common in Jan–Feb even on dry days. It is effectively a March–May destination for comfort.
- Sicily and Crete: the Jan–Mar mean max of about 16 °C hides spells of 11–13 °C with N winds and rain, alternating with scirocco days of 20 °C or more.

### Gaps
- No quantitative frequency statistics (e.g. "% of Jan–Feb weeks with ≥2 rain days") were found for any location. A proper answer would need daily station data (e.g. ECA&D, Meteostat), which could not be reached.

## Q5 – Recent trends (2020–2026) vs long-term normals

### Takeaway
Recent winters have been warmer than the 1991–2020 normal in Spain and the Mediterranean islands (Baleares +1.6 °C in winter 2025–26). The Canaries were close to normal. Warmer does not mean drier: winter 2025–26 in Spain was also very wet. The 2022–2024 period featured severe drought in Catalonia (Girona) and Andalusia, followed by wet 2025–2026. For trip planning, the current normals may underestimate typical temperatures in the Mediterranean by about 0.5–1.5 °C, but not in the Canaries.

### Cited Findings
- Peninsular Spain winter 2025–26 mean was 7.6 °C, +1.0 °C vs 1991–2020. It was the ninth-warmest winter since 1961 and very wet. Baleares were +1.6 °C ("muy cálido") and the Canaries +0.2 °C ("normal") — [MITECO/AEMET press release Mar 2026](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/marzo/el-invierno-tuvo-caracter-muy-calido-y-muy-humedo.html); [AEMET avance invierno 2025–26](https://www.aemet.es/documentos/es/noticias/2026/03/avance_climatico_nacional_invierno_2025_2026.pdf)
- AEMET: the last eight winters have been warmer than normal. 2025 was the third-warmest year on record, tied with 2024, and the last four years are the warmest in the series — [MITECO, Jan 2026](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/enero/2025-fue-un-ano-extremadamente-calido-y-con-caracter-humedo-en-c.html); [AEMET annual summary 2025](https://www.aemet.es/en/noticias/2026/01/resumen_anual_2025)
- Mallorca: January 2024 was the second-warmest January on record — [Majorca Daily Bulletin, Feb 2024](https://amp.majorcadailybulletin.com/weather/weather/2024/02/07/121053/mallorca-weather-january-was-the-second-warmest-record-mallorca.html)
- Mountain snow still happens in warm years: Teide closed in March 2026, Madeira mountains closed in March 2026, Troodos in Feb 2025 and May 2026 — see the Q3 sources

### Inferences
- When comparing, add about +0.5 to +1 °C to the 1981–2010 Mediterranean values (Mallorca, Costa Blanca, Málaga, Girona) to reflect 2018–2026 conditions. The Canaries' values can be used unchanged.
- Recent extreme wet events (DANA Valencia, Oct 2024) fall outside the Jan–May window but show the levante storm risk on the Costa Blanca, which is mainly an autumn risk.

### Gaps
- No 2020–2026 trend data were found for Cyprus, Greece/Crete, Italy/Sicily, Malta, Turkey, Morocco or Portugal/Madeira.
- AEMET winter 2024–25 figures were not found.
