# On-the-ground costs: 4–5 night road-cycling trip, 5–6 people, Jan–Apr 2027

Research date: 22 Oct 2026. Conversion: 1 EUR = 24.5 CZK. GBP/USD figures are given as found and not converted unless stated.

**How this was researched:** Rental-shop pages could not be opened directly because the sandbox proxy blocked fetches. All prices below come from web-search snippets of the shop or aggregator pages. The search engine often marked a page as "X days old", and I note that where it applies. Most rental pages carry no explicit year. Treat every price as indicative and confirm it before booking. Anything that is my own estimate sits under "Inferences" and is not a sourced fact.

---

## Q1: What are realistic 2026/27 on-the-ground prices per location (bike rental, accommodation, van/car, transfer, dinner, total)?

### Takeaway
A carbon road bike costs about €25–50 per day on a 4–5 day hire in most Spanish, Portuguese, Canarian, Cypriot and Sicilian locations. Girona, Marbella, Tenerife, Crete and premium Algarve bikes cost more, at €45–110 per day. Excluding flights, a self-organised group of 6 sharing a villa or apartment, with one van or two cars, should budget roughly **€550–850 per person for 4 nights** and **€700–1,050 per person for 5 nights** (≈13,500–26,000 CZK). Antalya and Marrakech can come out cheaper on bikes and food, but the Marrakech guided and supported option costs more.

## Q3: Peak-season effects (Mallorca/Calpe March–April, Easter 28 Mar 2027, pro-team camps)

### Takeaway
Calpe's pro-team and amateur camp peak is late January to February, while Mallorca peaks March to May. Bike rental and accommodation prices rise through spring. Easter 2027 (28 March) falls in Mallorca's busiest window, so book early. Canary winter rental rates (Oct–Apr) are the islands' high season.

### Cited findings
- Calpe rental prices are lowest Nov–Feb and climb through spring. — [bikesbooking Calpe](https://bikesbooking.com/th/rent-a-bicycle-in-Calpe/)
- Calpe is described as where "all the pros train in winter", and the camps cluster in late Jan to early Feb 2027. — [Sportive Breaks Calpe 2026](https://sportivebreaks.com/cycling-breaks/calpe-training-camps/), [Ride the Struggle 2027](https://ridethestruggle.com/products/cycling-training-camp-in-calpe-in-2027)
- The Roadman 2026 guide recommends March–May or Sept–Oct for Mallorca. It quotes €80–150 per night for a room with breakfast and €40–80 per day for bike hire. — [Roadman](https://roadmancycling.com/blog/mallorca-cycling-training-camp-guide)
- Ride the Struggle's 2026 Calpe and Mallorca camps were sold out. — [Ride the Struggle](https://ridethestruggle.com/collections/training-camps)
- CCT Puerto de Mogán's published rates are winter (Oct–Apr) rates; summer is cheaper. — [CCT Mogán](https://www.cctbikerental.com/rentals/puerto-de-mogan/)
- Van rental peak pricing (Jul–Aug) runs ~£150–210 per day vs £75–130 off-peak in Spain, so Jan–Apr counts as off-peak for vans. — [9seatercarhire Spain](https://www.9seatercarhire.org.uk/9-seater-car-hire-spain/)
- Hürzeler's published Mallorca rates are for 1 Jun–17 Sep 2026, implying separate spring pricing. — [Hürzeler](https://www.huerzeler.com/en/rad/cube-attain-budget)

### Inferences
- Expect Mallorca rental fleets and bike hotels to be tightest from mid-March to April 2027, especially around Easter (24–28 Mar 2027, Holy Week).
- Calpe is cheapest and least crowded in March–April, after the pro camps leave.
- Booking bikes 2–3 months ahead (by Jan 2027) is advisable for Mallorca, Calpe and Girona.
- Treat the Mallorca accommodation range as the upper end for Easter week.
- Girona's busy period is spring–summer, when pros are resident. January–February is quieter but colder.

### Gaps
- No quantified Easter 2027 price premium was found for hotels or rentals.
- No sourced data on whether pro teams block rental fleets.
