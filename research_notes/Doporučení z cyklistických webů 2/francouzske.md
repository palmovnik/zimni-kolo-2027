## Cyprus
- Best period February–June and September–December; left-hand traffic, quiet minor roads, good surface, but watch for potholes on descents — [Edelweiss, Cyclisme sur route à Chypre](https://www.flyedelweiss.com/ch/fr/destinations/tips/road-cycling/cyprus.html), date n/a
- Limassol–Larnaca coast is flat; hinterland harder and greener — same Edelweiss page / [Tour Magazin FR, Chypre tours de printemps](https://www.tour-magazin.de/fr/europe/ile-mediterraneenne-de-chypre-tours-de-printemps-a-velo-de-course-quatre-tours-de-velo-de-course-a-chypre/), date n/a
- Troodos: Mount Olympus (1,952 m) climb demanding but pleasant; gradients mostly moderate, ramps above 10% rare — same Edelweiss page, date n/a
- Warning: frequent speed bumps in villages (per a testimonial; Tour Magazin/TravelMole text) and stones on descents — [TravelMole, New cycle route for Cyprus](https://travelmole.com/news/new-cycle-route-for-cyprus) (EN), date n/a
- Cyprus is a good winter training-camp option through April, warmest part of Europe — [Tour Magazin FR, six alternatives à Majorque](https://www.tour-magazin.de/fr/tours/france/camps-d-entrainement-de-velo-de-course-au-printemps-six-alternatives-europeennes-a-majorque/), date n/a
- Quote: "Dans l'arrière-pays, les parcours deviennent plus exigeants et le paysage plus vert." (Czech gist: vnitrozemí je náročnější a zelenější.) — Tour Magazin FR (Chypre), date n/a
- No named rentals/hotels/restaurants found; no participant reviews found.

## Crete
- Best period February–April and September–November — [Edelweiss, Cyclisme sur route en Crète](https://www.flyedelweiss.com/ch/fr/destinations/tips/road-cycling/crete.html), date n/a
- Sfakia road likened to an alpine pass; avoid main arteries because drivers often take the centre of the road — same page, date n/a
- Hardest climb flagged: Lakkoi–Omalos, ~500 m to 1,100 m over 12 km, >1h45 — [Bikemap FR route 267393](https://www.bikemap.net/fr/c/267393/), date n/a
- Loop from Chora Sfakion 63.7 km / ~1,219 m; loop from Rethymno via Prasies, dam lake, Patsos, Spili, Armeni 57.9 km / ~1,147 m — [Bikemap EN route 267393](https://www.bikemap.net/en/c/267393/), date n/a (community tracks)
- Warning: forum post mentions roadworks Rodakino–Selia near Spili on Sfakia–Rethymno link, partly unpaved (unverified, old) — [Routard forum, Etat des routes](https://www.routard.com/forums/t/etat-des-routes/52354), date n/a
- Rethymno = base town candidate (guided e-bike tour from ~110 €, not road bikes) — [Manawa](https://www.manawa.com/fr/destinations/1608-rethymno/1551-velo), date n/a
- No named road-bike rentals/restaurants found.

## Morocco (Marrakech / High Atlas)
- Tizi n'Test summit 2,092 m, R203 section ~170 km; thick cloud/fog possible, signs at Marrakech/Taroudant exits say if closed by snow — [Frommer's, Tizi n'Test](https://www.frommers.com/destinations/western-high-atlas/attractions/tizi-ntest) (EN), date n/a
- Komoot bikepacking route Marrakech to Taroudant via Agafay, Ouirgane, N'fis valley, Tizi n'Test: 660+ km, 8 stages, ~8,250 m ascent — [Komoot collection](https://www.komoot.com/collection/1448411/morocco-of-the-passes-and-beaches-the-atlas-via-the-tizi-n-test) (EN), date n/a
- Warning: road described as vertiginous, start early, dangerous at night — [PressReader (Elle UK, 2017)](https://www.pressreader.com/uk/elle-uk/20171001/285065574276732) (EN), 2017-10-01
- Routard forum: old/isolated reports of roadworks; another says ordinary paved national road — [Routard forum](https://www.routard.com/forums/t/itineraire-au-maroc-faire-un-choix/169400), date n/a
- French operator Terdav lists only MTB/e-MTB in Ourika/Agafay, not road — [Terdav](https://www.terdav.com/ps-maroc/tp-circuit-accompagne/at-vtt/marv03--haut-atlas-vallee-ourika-vtt-electrique), date n/a
- No French road-cyclist trip report found.

## Jordan

## Oman
- Jebel Akhdar ("Green Mountain") is the Tour of Oman queen stage; 2019 edition: 5.7 km at 10.5% avg — [Cycling Weekly, Tour of Oman 2019 route](https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/latest-news/tour-oman-route-157203) (EN), 2019
- Past Green Mountain winners who took the GC: Lutsenko 2019, Hermans 2017, Nibali 2016, Valls 2015, Froome 2014, Gesink 2011 — [Wikipedia, 2016 Tour of Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_Tour_of_Oman) and sibling pages (EN), date n/a

## Lanzarote

## Fuerteventura
- Windiest of the Canaries; some north-south sections exhausting or dangerous; guided/marked routes advised — [Routard forum, Quelle île choisir pour faire du vélo aux Canaries](https://www.routard.com/forums/t/quelle-ile-choisir-pour-faire-du-velo-aux-canaries/287506), date n/a
- Forum member (EN): Corralejo in the north has the best rides; south (Costa Calma, Morro Jable) quieter with long straights; plan the windy leg first — [CycleChat thread](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/winter-cycling-destination.310901/post-7626847) (EN), date n/a

- Café: Blanca Bikes on the way to the col, popular with cyclists — Komoot guide above, date n/a
- Other climbs: Vall d'Ebo, Cumbre del Sol, flat loops to Denia — [Rouvy podcast page](https://rouvy.com/fr/blog/georgia-bullard-on-rouvy-unchained-podcast) (EN content), 2025-12-10
