# Weakly covered destinations - research notes

Method: WebSearch snippets only (27 of 45 searches used). WebFetch unavailable, so no page was opened; every claim comes from a search-result summary. Publication dates were mostly NOT shown in snippets ("date n/a"). Treat prices as indicative and verify.

## Tenerife
- Best months: sources disagree. A rental guide says May-June and Sep-Nov are best value and Dec-Mar and Jul-Aug are high season (prices up); a cycling blog says winter has best weather and fewer crowds; tourism board says autumn has pleasant temperatures and calmer coastal wind. For Jan-May 2027 expect high-season prices and possible snow at altitude. — [Bikesbooking Tenerife](https://bikesbooking.com/th/rent-a-bicycle-in-Tenerife/), [Cycling in Tenerife (webtenerife)](https://www.webtenerife.co.uk/what-to-do/sports/land/cycling/), date n/a
- Classic climb: Mount Teide (six main routes); 10Adventures runs a trip with 2 Teide ascents up to 2,350 m. — [10Adventures Tenerife road cycling](https://cms.10adventures.com/tour/tenerife-road-cycling-tour), [PJAMM Tenerife zone](https://pjammcycling.com/zone/397.Cycling-Tenerife-Canary-Islands), date n/a
- Granadilla-Vilaflor-Teide (TF-21) loop of 142 km; lower section less steep than the busier road above Vilaflor. — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/tenerife/mount-teide-cycling-granadilla-vilaflor/), date n/a
- Masca descent/ride "highly recommended"; road past Vilaflor is quiet, "you could well see more cyclists than vehicles". — [CycleChat Tenerife thread](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/tenerife.292471/), date n/a (older forum)
- Base: south coast (Los Cristianos / Costa Adeje) is where self-guided packages and hotel-delivery rentals concentrate. — [Sportive Breaks self-guided Tenerife](https://sportivebreaks.com/self-guided-road-cycling/tenerife), date n/a
- Rental/operators named: Bike Experience Tenerife (rents road and MTB, guided/self-guided; no price seen); Cycling Holidays Tenerife (bike hire + accommodation + guiding since 2015); Sportive Breaks (Pinarello, BMC, Scott road bikes, hotel 3-7 nights); CyclingSpain package from EUR 995 pp for 8 days incl. carbon road bike for 6 days (2025). — [webtenerife cycling holidays](https://www.webtenerife.co.uk/plan-trip/tourist-service-companies/activity-companies/cycling-holidays-tenerife/), [CyclingSpain](https://www.cyclingspain.com/?p=5361), 2025
- Rental prices: performance road bikes about EUR 20-35/day, weekly discounts (aggregator, not a shop price list); budget alloy bikes about EUR 11-12/day (undated blog). Carbon shop prices NOT found; Musement lists a Ridley Fenix carbon hire (4-8 h) without visible price. — [Bikesbooking](https://bikesbooking.com/th/rent-a-bicycle-in-Tenerife/), [Tenerife-training blog](https://www.tenerife-training.net/Tenerife-News-Cycling-Blog/?p=947), [Musement carbon hire](https://trenitalia.musement.com/uk/tenerife/pro-carbon-road-bike-hire-in-tenerife-1-258116/), date n/a
- Warning: roads into Teide National Park can close for snow/ice after winter storms (PJAMM cites a Feb 2026 closure of TF-21/TF-24; single source). Check Cabildo de Tenerife road status. — [PJAMM Mount Teide (Los Cristianos)](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/1887.Mount-Teide-Los-Cristianos), 2026
- Warning (second-hand, possibly outdated): after the 2023 fires some northern Teide routes may restrict cyclists. — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/tenerife/mount-teide-cycling-granadilla-vilaflor/), date n/a
- Warning: temperature can drop about 10 C through cloud below Vilaflor; carry arm/leg warmers and gilet. — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/tenerife.292471/), date n/a
- Quote: "you'll could well see more cyclists than vehicles" (on the road past Vilaflor). — [CycleChat](https://www.cyclechat.net/threads/tenerife.292471/post-7074545), date n/a
- Shop names Bike Point / Free Motion (suggested by earlier brief) were NOT confirmed by any result.

## Jordan
- Best months: NOT found by any source. Exodus mentions "hot temperatures" generally. — [Exodus Jordan trip notes](https://exodus.co.uk/tripnotes/mxw/85531), 2024
- Classic route: Mount Nebo - Madaba plateau - Dead Sea viewpoint - fast descent to the lowest point; descent from Husban (~800 m) about 20 km with gradients topping 20%; climb back out is a 25 km climb to the plateau at 900 m+. — [Skedaddle Jordan](https://www.skedaddle.com/uk/adventures/holiday/Cycling_Adventures/Jordan_-_Petra_Wadi_Rum__The_Dead_Sea_-_Cycling_Holiday/1038/view.rails), [Total Women's Cycling](https://totalwomenscycling.com/lifestyle/travel/cycling-jordan-a-guide-to-ten-days-in-this-magical-country), date n/a
- Base: Amman/Madaba (guided trips start in Amman). — [Intrepid Cycle Jordan](https://www.intrepidtravel.com/eu/jordan/cycle-jordan-petra-wadi-rum-dead-sea-167366/tripnotes), valid 2026
- Local shop/operator: Cycling Jordan (Amman) - Dead Sea day ride JOD 15 incl. bike, helmet, water, snacks, support car, guide (listing undated; tel. +962 7 8555 2525). Road/carbon bikes NOT confirmed. — [The Media Line listing](https://themedialine.org/?p=96680), date n/a
- Tour operators (multi-day, mostly hybrid/MTB, not road): SpiceRoads 8 days US$2,450 (+US$185 MTB hire) - [SpiceRoads](https://spiceroads.com/tours/jordan_trails/factsheet); Skedaddle 8 days from US$2,995 - [Skedaddle](https://www.skedaddle.com/us/adventures/holiday/Cycling_Adventures/Jordan_-_Petra_Wadi_Rum__the_Dead_Sea_-_Cycling_Holiday/1038/view.rails); Exodus ~45 km/day, 95% paved - [Exodus](https://exodus.co.uk/tripnotes/mxw/85531); 10Adventures 8 days ~200 km over 4 riding days - [10Adventures](https://www.10adventures.com/tour/jordan-petra-wadi-rum-cycling/); Fleewinter includes bike hire - [Fleewinter](https://www.fleewinter.com/jordan-holidays/the-cycling-tour/). Per-day price (derived): SpiceRoads about US$306/day. Dates n/a.
- Warnings: traffic on some stretches (one writer skipped a section); early starts recommended; packs of wild dogs mentioned once, no detail. — [Total Women's Cycling](https://totalwomenscycling.com/lifestyle/travel/cycling-jordan-a-guide-to-ten-days-in-this-magical-country), date n/a
- Quote: the Dead Sea to Mount Nebo route is "something serious road cyclists tend to enjoy". — [Culture Trip: Best places to cycle in Jordan](https://www.theculturetrip.com/articles/spectacular-places-to-cycle-in-jordan), date n/a
- "Bike Jordan" / "Experience Jordan" as named in the brief: NOT found in results.

## UAE (Ras Al Khaimah, Jebel Jais, Dubai Al Qudra, Hatta)
- Best months: winter (Dec-Mar) for Al Qudra; in summer ride before sunrise to 9:00. — [Sandy Times 2026 guide](https://sandytimes.ae/articles/3881/best-cycling-routes-in-dubai-and-beyond-2026-guide), 2026
- Jebel Jais: about 24 km from ~315 m to ~1,346 m, avg ~5.3%; Ras Al Khaimah tourism says gradients suit intermediate riders; many start 5:30-6:30; cafe at top. Other source: 32 km from entrance; "wide shoulders and smooth tarmac". — [Visit Jebel Jais cycling blog](https://visitjebeljais.com/blog/cycling-in-ras-al-khaimah-jebel-jais-trails-events), [Café du Cycliste: Jebel Jais](https://cafeducycliste.com/en/la-gazette/jebel-jais-a-mountain-in-the-desert), [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5442491), date n/a
- Base: Ras Al Khaimah (Al Marjan Island hotels sit on the coastal cycle track; most hotels described as bike-friendly, some rent bikes; Ritz-Carlton Al Wadi lets guests ride its reserve). — [Visit Ras Al Khaimah](https://visitrasalkhaimah.com/?p=88424), date n/a
- Rental: Wolfi's - Al Qudra rental point at Last Exit; website says from AED 50/hour; carbon road bikes AED 200/day per Dubizzle (helmet AED 50 for carbon); brands Colnago, Factor, Scott listed. — [Wolfi's Last Exit blog](https://wolfis.ae/blogs/our-blog/wolfis-bike-rental-at-last-exit-al-qudra), [Dubizzle rent cycle Dubai](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/cars/rent-cycle-dubai/), 2024-2026
- Rental: Trek Bicycle Store Al Qudra - sources conflict (full carbon AED 100/4 h, or AED 53/2 h weekday, 95 weekend). — [Property Finder bike rentals](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/bike-rentals/), [Dubizzle Al Qudra](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/cars/al-qudra-cycle-track/), 2024-2026
- Rental near Jais: Showka Bicycles (Hajar Mountains) - MTB rentals and tours only; road bikes in RAK NOT found. — [Visit Ras Al Khaimah](https://visitrasalkhaimah.com/?p=88424), date n/a
- Al Qudra: Last Exit start with food trucks, supermarket, fuel; no traffic lights; track is not lit after sunset; some segments unpaved. — [Dubizzle Al Qudra](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/cars/al-qudra-cycle-track/), [Komoot](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/7336535), date n/a
- Hatta: Dubai Municipality completed 21 MTB-oriented cycling routes (53 km) in Aug 2024; bikes rentable on site; noon sun strong (Komoot comment). Road-bike content for Hatta NOT found. — [Dubai Media Office, Aug 2024](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2024/august/22-08/mountain-trails-in-hat), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/winter-in-dubai-explore-hatta-on-a-mountain-bike-1.1699006252929)
- Warnings: Jebel Jais road has been closed for race days (UAE Tour 6:00-17:00) and after heavy rain; check RAK Police. — [Gulf News: rains close Jebel Jais](https://gulfnews.com/uae/transport/rains-cause-closure-of-road-to-uaes-highest-mountain-jebel-jais-1.92902019), [Gulf News: road closures UAE Tour](https://gulfnews.com/amp/uae/transport/caution-road-closures-to-take-place-in-uae-for-cycling-tour-1.1550569430821), 2019-2020 and later
- Quote: Jebel Jais is "one of the most rider-friendly mountain roads in the Gulf". — [Visit Jebel Jais](https://visitjebeljais.com/blog/cycling-in-ras-al-khaimah-jebel-jais-trails-events), date n/a
- Tour operators / cafes beyond Last Exit food trucks: NOT found.

## Oman (Muscat, Jebel Akhdar, Jebel Shams)
- Best months: European winter, around December-January; summer heat. — [Bicycling South Africa: Cycling Oman](https://www.bicycling.co.za/travel/cycling-oman/), date n/a
- Jebel Akhdar: about 14 km at ~10% avg, ~1,400 m gain; queen stage of Tour of Oman since 2011; "steepest paved road in Oman" per one rider. — [PJAMM Jabal al Akhdhar](https://pjammcycling.com/climb/1265.Jabal-al-Akhdhar), [Muscat Daily 2020](https://www.pressreader.com/oman/muscat-daily/20200120/281492163273755)
- Jebel Shams: top section said to rival Mont Ventoux/Alpe d'Huez; first 10 km newly paved at time of writing. — [Bicycling South Africa](https://www.bicycling.co.za/travel/cycling-oman/), date n/a
- Warning (key): ROP checkpoint after the first steep ramps lets only 4WD vehicles pass; a tour-operator account says bikes are "normally forbidden"; no source confirms current rules for cyclists - ask a local operator/ROP. Race stages have ridden it. — [Wikiloc Jabel Akdar](https://www.wikiloc.com/offroading-trails/jabel-akdar-28996926), [Bicycling Australia: Oman](https://bicyclingaustralia.com.au/places/travel-tales-oman-cycling-in-the-switzerland-of-the-middle-east/), date n/a
- Muscat: drivers "not very sympathetic" to cyclists, care at roundabouts; coastal road Muscat-Sur a popular ride. — [AskExplorer cycling Oman](https://askexplorer.com/oman/residents/activities/activities-hobbies/cycling), [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/oman/articles/the-best-cycling-routes-in-oman), date n/a
- Operator: Experience Oman 10-day Oman Cycling Tour from OMR 636 pp (Muscat, Wadi Dayqah, Nizwa, Jebel Shams via Al Hamra); about OMR 64/day derived. Responsible Travel 6-day tailor-made GBP 3,110 (min 2). Road-bike spec not stated. — [Experience Oman](https://booking.experienceoman.om/en/idea/54067192/oman-cycling-tour), [Responsible Travel](https://www.responsibletravel.com/holiday/16446/oman-cycling-and-trekking-holiday), date n/a
- Rental: Culture Trip says rental hubs exist (Muttrah Corniche); no named shop with road/carbon bikes found. "Cycle Oman" NOT found as a business.
- Quote: Jebel Akhdar "is the queen stage" - winner usually takes overall (Muscat Daily 2020). — [Muscat Daily](https://www.pressreader.com/oman/muscat-daily/20200120/281492163273755), 2020-01-20

## Georgia (Kakheti, Tbilisi, Gombori)
- Best months: Georgia Tourism suggests April-October. — [georgia.travel Kakheti wine route biking](https://georgia.travel/biking-along-the-kakheti-wine-route), date n/a. (Jan-Mar likely cold; not verified.)
- Gombori Pass: summit ~1,620 m, steep final ~10 km, hardest day of Tbilisi-Telavi tours; descent through forest into Kakheti. Bikemap advises early start to avoid traffic leaving Tbilisi. — [RACQ itinerary](https://travel.racq.com.au/cycling-in-georgia/overview), [Bikemap Tbilisi-Gombori-Telavi](https://www.bikemap.net/en/c/249078/), date n/a
- Kakheti: Gurjaani-Telavi via Tsinandali with Vazisubani Estate stop; Sighnaghi has 16 community routes incl. an 80.7 km road route. Roads mostly sealed, light traffic on backroads. — [georgia.travel](https://georgia.travel/biking-along-the-kakheti-wine-route), [SpiceRoads Georgia](https://www.spiceroads.com/tours/georgia-cycling-holiday/factsheet), date n/a
- Base: Telavi or Sighnaghi (tour overnight stops); Tbilisi busier. — [SpiceRoads itinerary](https://www.spiceroads.com/tours/georgia-cycling-holiday/itinerary), date n/a
- Operators: SpiceRoads 9 days US$2,950 (MTB hire US$280); Responsible Travel 9 days GBP 1,360-1,765 (40-75 km/day); TourRadar from US$1,950; Georgian Holidays day ride (MTB hire EUR 25). Mostly MTB/hybrid; road bikes NOT confirmed. — [SpiceRoads](https://www.spiceroads.com/tours/georgia-cycling-holiday/details), [Responsible Travel](https://www.responsibletravel.com/holiday/32455/cycling-in-georgia-holiday), [TourRadar](https://www.tourradar.com/t/158026), [Georgian Holidays](https://www.georgianholidays.com/index.php/tour/cycling-tours/bike-trip-to-turtle-lake-and-betania/pdf), date n/a
- Wine/food: the Kakheti wine-route framing (Vazisubani, Tsinandali) fits food-lovers, but no named cafes/restaurants were found.
- Warnings: Tbilisi exit traffic/pollution; dogs not covered.
- Quote: "the journey from Gurjaani to Telavi is an amazing day trip". — [georgia.travel](https://georgia.travel/biking-along-the-kakheti-wine-route), date n/a
