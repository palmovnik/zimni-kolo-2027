# Food, cycling cafés and wine stops (research notes)

Method: 44 WebSearch calls; WebFetch unavailable, so everything below comes from search-result snippets only, not from reading the full pages. Dates are given only where the snippet showed one ("date n/a" otherwise). Komoot "recommended by X road cyclists" figures are small user samples. Opening hours in Jan-Mar were NOT verified for any venue unless stated.

## Costa Blanca (Calpe / Dénia / Altea / Xaló)
- Velosol cycling café/bar in Xaló (coffee, repair workshop; Komoot calls it an obligatory stop on Marina Alta roads; used as the main café stop before Coll de Rates by a training-camp operator) — [Komoot highlight](https://blog.komoot.it/highlight/650539) and [Destination Sport Experiences, Calpe camp](https://destinationsportexperiences.com/event/Calpe-Cycling-Training-Camps---4-Night-Camp/1704), date n/a
- Epic Road Rides puts VeloSol in Jalón (not Xaló) and says it usually closes two weeks at end of August; verify location and winter hours — [Epic Road Rides route page](https://epicroadrides.com/?p=10355), date n/a
- Café de Rutes, Parcent (foot of Coll de Rates; Komoot reviewers praise the food, run by Austrians) — [Komoot highlight](https://blog.komoot.es/highlight/174183), date n/a
- Restaurante Tramonti in Parcent suggested as refuel at the bottom of the climb; a restaurant at the summit of Coll de Rates exists but does no takeaway or sandwiches — [Epic Road Rides](https://epicroadrides.com/?p=10355), date n/a
- D'Origin, Albir (just south of Altea): coffee shop, one of Spain's first micro-roasters; Epic's "coffee ride" Calpe-Albir out-and-back on the N-332 (54 km; the author dislikes the N-332 itself) — [Epic Road Rides, Coffee ride Serra Gelada](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/calpe-costa-blanca/coffee-ride-serra-gelada), date n/a
- Guadalest and Confrides have "a selection of places to stop"; Confrides-Pego is sparsely populated, carry food — [Epic Road Rides, Guadalest & Vall d'Ebo loop 138 km](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-spain/calpe-costa-blanca/guadelest-and-vall-debo-loop/), date n/a
- Tàrbena: a café just before the village is an ideal stop after the long climb (views over Bolulla valley); Komoot mentions a "cafe ciclista" in the last km of the climb; another good bar in Benasau on the CV-70 — [Road Cycling UK](https://roadcyclinguk.com/?p=10386) and [Komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/4613063), date n/a (RCUK article may be dated)
- La Esquina Café, Jávea (end of a 24.9 km Pedreguer vineyards loop on Komoot) — [Komoot smarttour](https://www.komoot.com/smarttour/32115873), page updated 23 May 2026
- Forum tips (older thread): Restaurante Columbus in Altea ("food is great"); L'Hort in Guadalest for lunch (views, good food) — [UKGSER thread](https://www.ukgser.com/community/threads/great-biking-area-in-eastern-spain-altea.336524/), date n/a
- Bodega Cooperativa Virgen Pobre (Bodegues Xaló), on the Xaló-Alcalalí road: coop winery founded 1962, tours/tastings almost daily, sells wine, olive oil, mistela; reviewer pairs it with L'Era restaurant in Jalón — [Denia.net, La Vall de Pop](https://denia.net/vall-de-pop1), date n/a
- Bodegas Gutiérrez de la Vega, Parcent: historic cellars (barrels in underground caves) — [Denia.net, La Vall de Pop](https://denia.net/vall-de-pop1), date n/a
- Calpe Michelin: Komfort (Cook Book hotel) is Bib Gourmand 2024 and 2025, price band €€, varied traditional/international, pizzas, cocas, rice — [Enprimeur Club listing](https://www.enprimeurclub.com/restaurants/komfort), date n/a
- Calpe has several starred places (Audrey's, Beat, Orobianco); Beat ranked third in Marina Alta behind Quique Dacosta (Dénia) and Bon Amb (Jávea) — [Olive Press, 12 Oct 2024](https://theolivepress.es/spain-news/2024/10/12/foodie-paradise-this-alicante-town-has-the-most-michelin-star-restaurants-per-capita-in-spain)

## Cyprus (Paphos / Limassol / Troodos)
- Epic Road Rides Mount Olympus loop from Paphos (152 km), stops listed: Extreme View Café km 31 and Oasis Café km 34 (early); convenience shop at Prodromos roundabout km 72; Ben Nevis Bar, Troodos Square km 84 (panoramic windows, good food); Orosimo Café, Pano Platres km 93; chocolate shop Kato Platres km 96 (~€0.50-1 per chocolate); cafés on the Omodos square facing the monastery km 102; cafés described as cheap and friendly — [Epic Road Rides, Cycling Mount Olympus Cyprus](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-cyprus/cycling-mount-olympus-cyprus/), date n/a
- Epic Road Rides argues Limassol suits winter riding: bike shops open year-round, and cafés serving residents stay open off-season; favourite Limassol-Troodos loop — [Epic Road Rides, Best winter cycling destinations Cyprus](https://epicroadrides.com/cycling-blog/best-winter-cycling-destinations-cyprus/), date n/a
- Limassol Komoot cafés: Bike & Bean Coffeestation (excellent coffee, sandwiches, sweets); George's Daughter & Son Café (review Nov 2025, friendly staff); Pedron Cafe (cake tip) — [Komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5535071), [Komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/5315944), [Komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/7169100), dates as stated
- Latikon Café at Stavros tis Psokas, recommended by 11 road cyclists on Komoot; official route notes a Kykkos restaurant stop before Kampos — [Komoot highlight](https://www.komoot.com/highlight/627147) and [Visit Cyprus, Troodos Square-Stavros tis Psokas](https://www.visitcyprus.com/?p=470950), date n/a
- Mountain tavernas (generic travel listing, NOT cyclist-specific): Makris Taverna, Moniatis (on Limassol-Platres road, fireplace, village sausages); Polydendri Taverna, Pelendri (family-run meze); Chrysanthis and Voreas, Kakopetria (meze, souvla, kleftiko) — [FastForward.com.cy, 5 Mountain Tavernas](https://fastforward.com.cy/life/5-mountain-tavernas-cyprus), date n/a
- Paphos area (generic): 7 St. George's Tavern, Geroskipou (family-run, organic, 20+ years); Yiangos & Peter, Latchi (harbour fish) — [Islaguru, best restaurants in Cyprus](https://islaguru.com/articles/best-restaurants-in-cyprus), date n/a
- Winter caveat: snow and cold on high Troodos roads reported (Cycling UK account; a March rider skipped Olympus) — [Cycling UK, Great Rides: Cyprus in the off season](https://www.cyclinguk.org/cycle/great-rides-cyprus-season), date n/a
- No Michelin/Bib Gourmand listing for Cyprus was found.
