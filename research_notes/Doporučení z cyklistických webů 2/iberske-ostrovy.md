- Visit Algarve official road-cycling guide (2024 PDF) suggests riding from Monchique up Picota then Fóia, resting at Caldas de Monchique — [Visit Algarve: Guia ciclismo estrada 2024 (EN)](https://api.visitalgarve.pt/uploads/1/1/Media/Brochuras/EN_Guia_ciclismo_estrada_2024.pdf), English, 2024

- LP-4 (Roque de los Muchachos) is routinely closed for snow/ice/rockfall by the Cabildo; partial reopenings may allow traffic only to Pico de la Nieve — [Cabildo: cierra la carretera del Roque por precaución ante la nieve](https://www.cabildodelapalma.es/es/el-cabildo-cierra-la-carretera-del-roque-de-los-muchachos-por-precaucion-ante-la-nieve), Spanish, undated; [elDiario.es: cerrada por nieve](https://www.eldiario.es/canariasahora/lapalmaahora/sociedad/cerrada-carretera-roque-muchachos-precaucion-nieve_1_11923391.html), Spanish, undated; [El Apurón: cierre entre Pico de la Nieve y la Residencia](https://elapuron.com/noticias/sociedad/173288/cabildo-cierra-la-carretera-del-roque-pico-la-nieve-la-residencia-la-nieve/), Spanish, undated. - LP-4 2026: I found NO confirmed 2026 closure/roadworks. - LP-2 (Las Manchas - Tajuya) reconstruction over 30% done in Aug 2026, completion expected in first four months of 2027 (could affect west/south connections in early 2027) — [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/las-obras-de-la-lp-2-en-la-palma-superan-el-30-de-ejecucion-04-agosto-2026/), Spanish, 4 Aug 2026; start: [RTVC 29 Dec 2025](https://rtvc.es/comienzan-las-obras-para-recuperar-la-lp-2-y-actualizar-la-lp-5-en-la-palma-29-diciembre-2025/)

- Rentals: Emotion Cycling lists road-bike rental (price not found); Bike 'n' Fun (Los Llanos, mostly MTB, 40 Bergamont), Magic Bike (Los Llanos), Bike Station (Puerto Naos) mainly MTB/e-bike — [Cycling Spain: Bike 'n' Fun](https://www.cyclingspain.com/?p=6926), English, undated; [la-palma24](https://www.la-palma24.net/la-palma-info/en/sports/mountainbiken). ## Fuerteventura
- Best asphalt variety is inland: Betancuria, Pájara, central hills; Puerto del Rosario - Morro Jable about 146 km / 1,790 m is a reference route; Corralejo loops of 46 km (Popcorn Beach) and 53 km (El Cotillo lighthouse) — [Hello Canary Islands: Cycle route in Fuerteventura](https://www.hellocanaryislands.com/cycling/fuerteventura/cycle-route-in-fuerteventura/), English, undated; [Pro Cycling UK: Cycling in Fuerteventura](https://procyclinguk.com/cycling-in-fuerteventura-why-it-deserves-a-place-on-your-riding-list/), English, undated
- Wind: trade winds frequent; a Corralejo rider described strong north winds in November and April, and sand on the road near dunes; south has long stretches without services (carry food/water) — summary of [Fuerteventura Guide](https://www.fuerteventuraguide.com/?p=409) and search results, English, undated (low certainty on exact source of the November/April remark)
- Cabildo resurfaced FV-30 (Valle de Santa Inés - Betancuria) with more than 360,000 EUR; 27 km of Betancuria/La Oliva/Puerto del Rosario roads also resurfaced — [Cabildo de Fuerteventura: FV-30](https://www.cabildofuer.es/cabildo/el-cabildo-invierte-mas-de-360-000-euros-en-el-arreglo-de-la-carretera-fv-30-desde-el-valle-de-santa-ines-hasta-betancuria/), Spanish, undated
- Faro Fuerteventura sportive crosses the island north-south (118 km / 1,800 m in one edition, via Betancuria) — [Cabildo: Faro Fuerteventura](https://www.cabildofuer.es/cabildo/vuelve-la-prueba-ciclodeportiva-faro-fuerteventura-con-un-recorrido-de-mas-de-110-kilometros/), Spanish, undated
- Rentals: results showed only MTB/e-bikes (Corralejo office on Calle Bocinegro, 50 EUR cash deposit); Easy Riders (Corralejo) and Volcano Bike (Costa Calma / Morro Jable) named; no road-bike price found — [Musement listing](https://www.musement.com/uk/fuerteventura/mountain-bike-rental-service-from-corralejo-in-fuerteventura-4-482776/), English, undated. Road-bike supply is the weak point on this island.

## Tenerife
- Teide access roads TF-21/TF-24 closed repeatedly for snow/ice in Feb-Mar 2026; Izaña gusts above 100 km/h in one storm; Cabildo asks to check its road-information centre; ice plates possible early morning after reopening — [Diario de Avisos: Teide vuelve a abrir (Mar 2026)](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/03/reapertura-carreteras-teide-tenerife-tf21-tf24/), Spanish, Mar 2026; [Diario de Avisos: nevada obliga a cerrar accesos (Feb 2026)](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/02/nieve-teide-accesos-cerrados/), Spanish, Feb 2026; [Diario de Avisos: cierre y alerta de viento](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/02/tenerife-cierre-accesos-teide-alerta-viento-nieve/), Spanish, Feb 2026.
